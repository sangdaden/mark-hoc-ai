Spectrogram and mask

The machine turns sound into a spectrogram: time across, pitch up the side. The model gives every cell a number from 0 to 1 called the mask: the voice's share of that cell.

Three takeaways

1Sound becomes a picture

The machine cuts sound into thousands of cells by pitch and time.

2One mask per cell

The model guesses the voice's share of each cell, learned from songs with separate tracks.

3Splitting isn't owning

Singing at home isn't posting online. Only split recordings you have the rights to.

Try it: record yourself singing over background music, open the vocal-separation feature in an audio app, and listen for what leaks into the vocal track.
