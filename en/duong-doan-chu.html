The Next-Word Road

Mark writes a sentence by jumping onto word platforms, just like a chatbot guessing the next word. Wide platforms are common words. Change the temperature and see whether the sentence gets more creative or more made up.

How to play: ← → or A D to run, Space or ↑ to jump, T to change the temperature. On a phone the game switches to landscape full screen; hold your phone sideways and use the on-screen buttons.

The odds on the platforms are simplified examples. Progress stays in this browser.
