The Next-Word Road

Mark fishes one word at a time to write a sentence, just like a chatbot guessing the next word. Common words come in big schools. Stir the temperature and see whether the sentence gets more creative or more made up.

How to play: tap or click the lake to cast there, Space to cast, T to change the temperature. On a phone the game switches to landscape full screen; hold your phone sideways and tap the lake.

The fish odds are simplified examples. Progress stays in this browser.
