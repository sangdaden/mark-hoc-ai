A lost passport in a strange place

Getting lost or losing your passport abroad is not the time to start worrying. Ask AI to help you prepare before the trip.

PHRASES

The phrases you need most

Ask AI for phrases in the local language for being lost, needing a doctor or losing your passport, with pronunciation.

CHECKLIST

An emergency list

Ask AI for an emergency list: a copy of your passport, your insurance number, your hotel address and the steps to take if documents are lost.

CHECK

Embassy numbers

Look up embassy numbers on the official site yourself, because AI can be wrong, and never paste your passport number into AI.

Try it now

Five emergency phrases

Ask AI for five emergency phrases in the language of your next destination, and save them in your notes.
