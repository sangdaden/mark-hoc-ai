Copy the answer, stuck again tomorrow

You ask AI a math problem, and it solves it right away, neat and correct. You copy it down and nod. The next day you get a similar problem, and you still can't do it.

THE HARDEST PART

AI does the thinking for you

The problem is that AI did the hardest part for you: the thinking. And that's exactly where learning happens.

SOCRATIC METHOD

Asking back so you think

Over two thousand years ago, Socrates taught by asking questions back, so his students would work out the answers themselves. This is called the Socratic method.

STUDY MODE

Study mode

Now the big AIs all have a mode like that: ChatGPT has Study mode, Gemini has Guided Learning, Claude has learning mode. AI gives hints and asks you questions, instead of handing over the answer.

DO IT YOURSELF

One prompt is enough

If your app doesn't have this mode, no problem. One prompt is enough: don't give me the answer, ask me step by step so I can figure it out myself.

WHEN TO USE IT

Understanding, or just the result?

Use it when you want to understand, like studying or preparing for exams. When you just need a quick result, like converting units, ask directly.

Try it now

The problem you're stuck on

Pick an exercise you're stuck on, turn on study mode or paste the prompt: don't give me the answer, ask me step by step. Answer each question, and see how far you get on your own.
