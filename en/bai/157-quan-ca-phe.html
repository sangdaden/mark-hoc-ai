How to use AI in a small café or restaurant

How to use AI in a small café or restaurant: no marketing team, but the owner can still let AI take over some daily paperwork.

Watch videoVideo in Vietnamese

A small café using AI

A small café has no marketing team, but it can ask AI to take over some of the daily paperwork.

MENU

Write a tempting menu

Give AI the dish names, ingredients and what makes each special, and ask for short menu descriptions, including an English version for foreign guests.

STOCK

Estimate ingredients

Paste last month's daily cup sales and ask AI which days are busy and which drinks sell best, so you buy just enough milk and pastries.

REVIEWS

Reply to customer reviews

When a customer complains online, ask AI for a polite draft reply, then add your own sincere apology.

Try it now

A menu card for the counter

Pick three best-selling items, ask AI for menu descriptions in two languages, then print a small card for the counter.
