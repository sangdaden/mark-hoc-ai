Day 4: fixing things at home

Day four of seven: a dripping tap or a noisy fan. Many small problems you can fix yourself with your phone camera and AI.

CLEAR PHOTO

Photograph the problem clearly

Take a close-up of the broken part, send it to a chatbot with a description of the noise or the leak, and ask for the usual causes.

STEP BY STEP

Photo after each step

Ask AI to guide you step by step. After each step, send a new photo so it can check before you go on.

SAFETY

Unplug it first

Always unplug the power or close the valve before taking anything apart. Wiring inside walls or a gas tank means calling a professional.

Try it now

Day 4 task

Photograph something at home that isn't working right, send it to a chatbot and ask for three safety checks.
