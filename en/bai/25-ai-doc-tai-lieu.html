What is RAG? How to let AI read your own documents

AI has read a whole library, but never your contract, course book or company rules, so it can only guess about them.

Watch videoVideo in Vietnamese

Your own documents

AI has read a whole library, but it has never read your contract, your course book or your company rules. Ask about them, and it can only guess.

Give it the document

The fix is to give AI the document. For long documents, the system cuts them into many small chunks.

Chunks on the map of meaning

Each chunk is placed on the map of meaning we met in part two. The chunk about the deposit sits in one place, the chunk about repairs sits in another.

Find the right chunk

When you ask a question, your question is placed on the map too. The system picks the chunks closest to your question.

Read first, then answer

Then AI reads those chunks before it answers. It relies on what's written instead of guessing, so it makes things up much less. This is called RAG.

Try it now

Only use the document

Upload a document, like your washing machine manual, then write: "Answer only from this document, and give the page number." Remember to open that page to check, and don't upload papers with sensitive information.

Problems with many steps

Now it has your documents, but what about hard problems with many steps? AI recently learned to think before it speaks. The last part explains.
