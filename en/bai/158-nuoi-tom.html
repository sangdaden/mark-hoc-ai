AI in shrimp farming: cameras that count shrimp, oxygen sensors

AI in shrimp farming: feeding by guesswork leads to leftover feed, dirty water and sick shrimp.

Watch videoVideo in Vietnamese

Feeding by guesswork

Feed shrimp by guesswork and you easily overfeed, the water gets dirty, and the shrimp get sick.

COUNTING

Cameras count and measure shrimp

Some devices use cameras and AI to count shrimp and measure their size, so the amount of feed can be worked out more precisely.

SENSORS

Sensors warn about bad water

Sensors in the pond measure oxygen and pH day and night, and when the readings get worse, they alert your phone so you can act in time.

LOGS

Ask AI about your log book

Without expensive equipment, farmers can still keep daily notes and ask a chatbot to find patterns, but an aquaculture engineer should check the conclusions.

Try it now

Two weeks of notes

Write down feed amounts, weather and water readings every day for two weeks, then ask AI to make a table and point out unusual days.
