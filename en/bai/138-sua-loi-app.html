What to tell AI when your app breaks: getting AI to fix bugs

What do you tell AI when your app breaks? Just typing "the app is broken" leaves AI guessing. How to get AI to fix the right bug.

Watch videoVideo in Vietnamese

Don't just say it's broken

Type just one line, the app is broken, and AI can only guess.

THREE THINGS

Tell it three things

Tell it three things: what you tapped, what you expected to see, and what actually happened.

ERRORS

Paste the whole error message

If there's a red error message, take a screenshot or paste it exactly as it is for AI, don't summarize it yourself.

SAVE A VERSION

Keep a version that works

Whenever the app works well, save a copy. If the next fix breaks it, go back to the old version without starting over.

Try it now

A bug report template

Save this template: I tapped, I expected, but actually, plus a screenshot, and ask for only one bug fix at a time.
