What is AI? Artificial intelligence explained simply for beginners

AI is all around you

You use AI every day without noticing: suggested videos, unlocking with your face, a keyboard that fixes your typos.

DEFINITION

A machine doing the work of a mind

AI, short for Artificial Intelligence, is a computer that can do things that used to need a human mind.

HOW IT LEARNS

Learning from examples

Instead of writing every rule by hand, people show the machine lots of examples so it works out the rules on its own.

TWO KINDS

AI that predicts, AI that creates

Some AI is built to predict, like filtering spam, while generative AI writes text and draws pictures on its own.

REMEMBER

Smart, but sometimes wrong

AI is smart but can be wrong with total confidence, so always double-check anything important.

Try it now

Ask about a hard word

Try it now: ask a chatbot to explain a hard word from your line of work, as if talking to a fifth grader.
