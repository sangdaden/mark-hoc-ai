Write leave requests and short job applications with AI

Write leave requests and short job applications with AI: they get sent back for missing a small detail, not for bad writing.

Watch videoVideo in Vietnamese

Sent back

Leave requests get sent back not because they're badly written, but because they're missing the dates, the reason or who covers for you.

LEAVE

Four points of a leave request

A leave request needs four points: the dates, a short reason, who covers for you, and how to reach you in an emergency.

APPLICATION

A three-sentence application

An application to a local shop needs just three sentences: who you are, what you can do, and when you're free.

ASK AI

You give the points, AI writes

Give a chatbot bullet points and ask for a polite, half-page letter. Fill in your name and phone number yourself afterwards.

Try it now

Write one letter

Type four points for a leave request, ask a chatbot to turn it into a letter, then double-check the dates.
