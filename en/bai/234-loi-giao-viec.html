Write a brief for an AI agent so it doesn't go wrong

Writing a task prompt for an AI agent: agents usually go wrong because the brief is vague. A good brief has three parts.

Watch videoVideo in Vietnamese

A vague brief

Agents usually go wrong because the brief is vague. A good brief has three parts: the goal, the limits, and when to ask back.

GOAL

Describe the result

The goal is a concrete result, like a table comparing five restaurants near the office, with prices and opening hours.

LIMITS

Draw the lines

Limits are what it must not do, like no booking and no going over budget.

ASK BACK

When to stop and ask

Finally, tell the agent to stop and ask you when information is missing or when it needs to log in.

Try it now

Write all three parts

Write a three-part brief for a small task, then ask a chatbot to point out what's still vague.
