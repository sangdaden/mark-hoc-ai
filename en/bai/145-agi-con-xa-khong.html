Guessing the year of AGI

Ask top experts what year AGI will arrive, and their answers are a decade apart.

WHY

People mean different things

Partly because everyone means something different by AGI, and partly because AI is improving very fast, but unevenly.

STILL MISSING

What AI still lacks

AI today is still weak at three things: learning on its own after training, being reliably right for days in a row, and working in the real world.

FOR YOU

Skills that still count

Whether AGI comes early or late, skills like asking the right questions, checking results and making decisions are still very valuable.
