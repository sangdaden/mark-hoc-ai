Plan projects and milestones with AI

Plan projects and milestones with AI, so a project doesn't slip just because the plan only had an end date.

Watch videoVideo in Vietnamese

Late from day one

Projects slip because the plan only lists the end date, and nobody writes down the steps in between.

MILESTONES

Work back from the deadline

Give a chatbot the goal and deadline, and ask it to split the work into milestones counted back from the deadline to today.

OWNERS

One person per milestone

Each milestone needs an owner and a specific date. Ask AI for a table to paste into the team chat.

BUFFER

Leave room for delays

Ask AI which task is most likely to slip, then add a few buffer days. AI doesn't know the team's time off, so add that yourself.

Try it now

Four milestones this month

Take something that must be done this month and ask AI to split it into four milestones with dates.
