Opening up AI's brain

You use AI every day, but what is it really doing inside? This eight-part series opens up AI's brain. Part one: AI doesn't read words the way you do.

Scissors that cut sentences

Before it understands anything, AI cuts your sentence into small pieces called tokens. Some pieces are a whole word, some are just part of a word.

Each piece becomes a number

Then each piece is turned into a number. What AI actually sees is a list of numbers, not letters.

Why AI miscounts letters

That's why AI often miscounts letters. It doesn't look at each letter, it looks at whole pieces. Letters hidden inside a piece are easy to miss.

Some languages take more pieces

Languages with accents, like Vietnamese, often get cut into smaller bits than English. The same idea takes more pieces. And how much AI can read, and how much you pay, are both counted in pieces.

Try it now

Split the letters, then count

When you need AI to count or rearrange letters, tell it to "split the word into single letters first, then count". And keep your requests short and clear, so AI uses fewer pieces but still gets the full idea.
