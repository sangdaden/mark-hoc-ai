Practicing oral exams with AI like a real examiner

Oral exams

Oral exams are scary because you don't know what the examiner will ask, but you can ask AI to play the examiner and practice first.

ROLE-PLAY

Make AI the examiner

Tell AI: you're the examiner for this subject, ask me one question at a time, wait for my answer before asking the next, and dig into anywhere my answer is vague.

VOICE

Practice out loud

Use voice chat mode to practice saying it out loud, because thinking it in your head and saying it are very different.

FEEDBACK

Ask for feedback at the end

At the end, ask AI to grade each answer and point out what you missed, then review exactly those parts.

Try it now

Fifteen minutes of oral practice

Pick three chapters for the exam, make AI the examiner, and answer out loud for fifteen minutes without looking at your notes.
