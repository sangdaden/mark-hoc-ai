What is an AI benchmark? What "scored 90%" really means

AI has finished learning, but how do we know how good it is?

How good is AI?

AI has finished learning. But how do we know how good it is? Every ad brags: our new AI scored 90%. Where does that number come from?

BENCHMARKS

AI takes tests too

AI takes tests too, like students taking final exams. AI's tests are called benchmarks: thousands of questions with answers ready, and every AI gets the same test.

SCORING

The score is the percent correct

Grading is simple: compare AI's answers with the answer key. Get 900 out of 1,000 right, and the score is 90%.

MANY SUBJECTS

A test for each subject

Some tests cover knowledge in many subjects, from history and medicine to law. Some are math tests, solved step by step. Some are coding tests: AI writes code, and a computer runs it to see if it works.

NOTE

One test, one skill

But each test measures only one skill. Getting an A in math doesn't mean you write well. An AI that's great at coding tests may not write good emails.

READING THE NUMBER

90% on which test?

So 90% on one test can't be compared with 90% on another. The number only means something when you know what the test is.

Try it now

Ask two questions back

Next time you see an ad saying an AI scored 90%, ask two questions. What was the test about? And is that like what I usually ask AI to do?
