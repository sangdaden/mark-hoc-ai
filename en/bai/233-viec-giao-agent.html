Which tasks to hand to an AI agent, and which not yet

What is an AI agent and what should you give it: the common mistake is giving it the wrong task. An agent is AI that does several steps for you.

Watch videoVideo in Vietnamese

Handing over the wrong task

The common mistake with AI agents is handing over the wrong task. An agent, AI that does several steps for you, is good at clear tasks that are easy to check.

HAND OVER

Tasks to hand over

Hand over tasks where mistakes can be fixed, like gathering information, comparing prices or drafting.

NOT YET

Tasks not to hand over yet

Don't hand over tasks that are hard to undo, like paying, emailing clients, deleting files or deciding for you.

THREE TESTS

The three-part test

The test has three parts: you can describe the result, you can check it in minutes, and a mistake won't cost much.
