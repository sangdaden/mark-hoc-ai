How to get AI to read photos: bills, prescriptions and confusing papers

A power bill that's strangely high and impossible to read? Just take a photo, send it to AI, and ask like you'd ask a person.

A bill you just can't figure out

This month's power bill is unusually high, and no matter how many times you read it, you can't see how they got that number.

Take one photo, ask one question

Now you can just take a photo, send it to AI, and ask like you'd ask a real person. It can read words, numbers and tables in the photo.

INSIDE AI

How does AI see a picture?

To AI, a picture is cut into many small squares, and each square is like a word. It reads those squares together with your question, then answers.

WHAT TO USE IT FOR

Just snap and ask

Bills, contracts, menus in another language, charts, even the error light on your washing machine: just snap a photo and ask.

NOTE 1

Clear photos get read correctly

The clearer and straighter the photo, the more accurately AI reads it.

NOTE 2

Important matters: ask an expert

For medicine or legal papers, AI helps you understand fast, but the final call should still come from an expert. And cover account numbers and ID numbers before you take the photo.
