Avoid tourist traps and overcharging by asking AI to summarize reviews

Avoid tourist traps and overcharging by asking AI to summarize reviews: a five-star place can still be a trap, because the average hides the complaints.

Watch videoVideo in Vietnamese

Five stars can still be a trap

A place with five-star reviews can still be a tourist trap, because the average score hides the complaints.

SUMMARY

Group the praise and complaints

Paste a few dozen recent reviews into AI and ask what people praise, what they complain about, and which complaint repeats most.

TRAPS

Common tricks

Ask AI to list common tricks where you're going, like taxis without a meter or menus without prices.

RECENT

Prefer recent reviews

Focus on reviews from the last few months, because the place may have changed owners, and AI can mix up old information.

Try it now

A place you plan to visit

Copy the 20 latest reviews of a place you plan to visit and ask AI to summarize the complaints that repeat.
