Where does AI learn from? Training and AI's knowledge cutoff date

When AI's brain is first built, it knows nothing. Then it's given a huge amount of text to read: web pages, books, articles, even computer code.

Watch videoVideo in Vietnamese

A brain just put together

We have the machine: cutting into pieces, the map of meaning, attention. But when it's just built, it knows nothing at all.

Reading a whole library

Then people let it read. A huge amount of text: web pages, books, articles, even computer code. More than one person could read in a thousand lifetimes.

Learning by guessing words

The way it learns is simple: hide the next word and make it guess. If it's wrong, it adjusts a little. Repeat billions of times, and it picks up grammar, facts and even how to reason.

The cutoff date

But the reading has to stop on a certain day, called the cutoff date. AI knows nothing about what happened after that day.

What about the latest news?

So if you ask about today's gold price, a new law or this week's news, AI may answer with old information, and still sound very sure.

Try it now

Ask for the cutoff date

Ask AI "how up to date is your knowledge?". For news, prices and new rules, turn on web search or paste a source for AI to read.

It can only continue sentences

After reading the whole library, AI only knows how to continue a sentence, not how to answer questions. So how did it become a polite assistant? Part five has the answer.
