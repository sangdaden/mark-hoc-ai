Write Facebook posts with AI that don't sound like a machine

Write Facebook posts with AI without sounding like a machine: AI posts give themselves away with clichés and emojis everywhere.

Watch videoVideo in Vietnamese

You can tell AI wrote it

Facebook posts written by AI are easy to spot: empty clichés and emojis scattered everywhere.

BRIEF IT

Tell AI to write like you talk

Tell AI to write as if telling a friend: short sentences, everyday words, just one or two emojis.

CUT

Cut the clichés

Read the draft and cross out anything that sounds like an ad, especially the hype at the start and the call to action at the end.

REAL STORY

Add your own story

Add one real detail from your day, like a meal or someone you met, and the post comes alive.

Try it now

Read it out loud

Ask a chatbot for a draft post, read it out loud, and rewrite any sentence you'd never actually say.
