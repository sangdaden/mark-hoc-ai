What is AI hallucination? Why AI makes things up and still sounds confident

Ask AI for 3 good books, and it answers right away, very sure. Look them up, and one doesn't exist.

Watch videoVideo in Vietnamese

The book that doesn't exist

You ask AI for three good books. It answers right away, very sure of itself. You look them up, and one of them doesn't exist.

This has a real name

This is called AI hallucination: a wrong answer that sounds true.

Why does it make things up?

Remember the last video: AI guesses the next word that sounds most likely. When it doesn't know, it doesn't stop. It still guesses a name that sounds very real.

Where it happens most

It happens most with numbers, book titles, laws and links.

WAY 1

Let AI say "I don't know"

One way to cut it down: add "if you're not sure, say you don't know".

WAY 2

Ask for sources, then check

And ask for sources. Click them and check. If a link doesn't open, don't trust it.

Try it now

Add these two sentences

Next time you ask for numbers or important facts, add those two sentences. AI is a great assistant, but you're the one who checks in the end.

If you're not sure, say you don't know. Give me sources so I can check.
