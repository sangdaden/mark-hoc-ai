Editing product photos with AI: background removal, lighting, virtual models

Photos taken on the bed, with a messy background and poor light, make shoppers scroll past. AI helps Mark redo his product photos.

Watch videoVideo in Vietnamese

Photos taken at home

Mark photographs his silk scarf right on the bed, with a messy background and dim light. It doesn't make anyone want to buy.

REMOVE BACKGROUND

Remove and replace the background

Bit shows him: AI tools now remove the background in one click, then place the product on a clean white background, or a scene that suits the customer.

LIGHTING

Fix the light, sharpen

AI also fixes brightness, balances color and sharpens blurry photos, work that used to need someone who knew photo editing.

MODEL PHOTOS

Virtual model photos

You can make photos of a virtual model using the product, so shoppers can picture it. It's best to say clearly that it's an illustration.

DON'T MISLEAD

Photos must match the real item

The most important rule: photos must look like the real item. Change the color or over-beautify, and customers will be disappointed when it arrives, return it and leave bad reviews.

Try it now

Redo your main photo

Reshoot your main product on a plain background with window light, then use AI to remove the background and fix the light for your main photo.
