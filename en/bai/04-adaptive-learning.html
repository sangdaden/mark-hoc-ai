What is adaptive learning? Turn AI into your own private tutor

When everyone learns at the same speed, fast learners get bored and slow learners fall behind.

Watch videoVideo in Vietnamese

Same lesson, same speed

The whole class learns the same lesson at the same speed. Fast learners get bored, and slower learners fall behind.

What is adaptive learning?

Adaptive learning is when a machine learns how you learn. It's like everyone having a private tutor who teaches exactly where you are.

THE SECRET

A 4-step loop

The secret is a loop. The system asks you a question and watches whether you answer right or wrong, fast or slow. Then it guesses your level and picks the next question to match.

Right means harder, wrong means step back

Get it right, and the questions get harder. Get it wrong, and it goes back to the basics you're missing. Words you tend to forget come back just before you would forget them.

You're already using it

Most of the language apps, test-prep apps and vocabulary apps you use run on this loop.

TIP

Do it yourself with an AI chat

You don't need a special app. You can turn an AI chat into an adaptive tutor with one message.

Try it now

Turn AI into your tutor

Tell AI: be my tutor for a topic. Ask me one question at a time. If I'm right, make it harder. If I'm wrong, explain and ask an easier one. After ten questions, tell me what I'm weak at.

Be my tutor for ____. Ask me one question at a time. If I get it right, make the next one harder. If I get it wrong, explain briefly and ask an easier one. After 10 questions, tell me which parts I'm weak at.
