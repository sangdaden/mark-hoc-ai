Use AI to read and summarize hundreds of customer reviews

Use AI to read and summarize hundreds of customer reviews, so you don't remember only a few harsh complaints and miss what many people say.

Watch videoVideo in Vietnamese

Three hundred reviews at once

Reading three hundred customer reviews by eye, you tend to remember a few harsh complaints and miss what many people say.

REMOVE NAMES

Delete customer details

Before pasting into AI, delete customers' names, phone numbers and order numbers. Keep only the feedback.

GROUP

Group by theme

Ask a chatbot to group the feedback by theme, count the comments in each group, and include two verbatim quotes.

RECOUNT

Double-check the numbers

AI can miscount or merge the wrong groups. Recount one group yourself before you report.

Try it now

Twenty comments

Paste twenty comments from under your post and ask AI to group them into three main themes.
