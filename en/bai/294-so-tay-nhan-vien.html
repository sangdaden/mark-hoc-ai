Lost on day one

New hires often quit early because nobody showed them anything on day one. A two-page handbook helps them feel settled.

What goes in the handbook

The handbook covers hours, how to request leave, how pay works, the main rules, and who to ask for help.

FIRST WEEK

A first-week plan

Add a first-week plan: day one, watch and help; day three, work with someone alongside; end of the week, sit down and check in.

ASK AI

From notes to handbook

Dictate messy notes into your phone and ask a chatbot to arrange them into a handbook with clear sections. Don't paste passwords or the shop's account numbers.
