Five small habits

Using AI safely doesn't require tech skills, just five small habits repeated every day.

HABITS 1-2

Hide details, check sources

One, delete names, ID numbers and account numbers before pasting. Two, ask for sources and open them before trusting a number.

HABITS 3-4

Fence in agents, stop at traps

Three, don't let agents pay or send emails on their own. Four, when you see easy-job high-pay offers, strange QR codes or apps asking to read your texts, stop.

HABIT 5

Experts decide

Five, for money, contracts and official papers, AI helps you understand, but a qualified person decides.
