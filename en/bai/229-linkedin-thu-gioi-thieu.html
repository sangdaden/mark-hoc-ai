Write your LinkedIn profile and cover letter with AI

Write your LinkedIn profile with AI: a profile with only a job title and a school name is almost invisible to recruiters.

Watch videoVideo in Vietnamese

An invisible profile

A LinkedIn profile with only a job title and a school name is almost invisible to recruiters searching for people.

HEADLINE

One line that says who you are

Ask AI for five headline versions, each saying clearly what you can do and for whom.

ABOUT

Three tasks, three results

In the About section, list three things you've done with their results, then ask AI to turn them into a short paragraph.

SHORT LETTER

A cover letter

A letter to a recruiter should be short, name the company correctly, and give one concrete reason you fit the role.

Try it now

Rewrite your About

Paste your current About section into a chatbot, ask for three shorter rewrites, then pick one.
