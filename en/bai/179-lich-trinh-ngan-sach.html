Plan a relaxed trip itinerary on a budget with AI

Plan a trip itinerary on a budget with AI without cramming five or six stops into a day and rushing past everything.

Watch videoVideo in Vietnamese

Too many stops

A common mistake when asking AI for an itinerary is five or six stops a day: all rushing, no enjoying.

THE DETAILS

Days, money, pace

Tell AI how many days, your budget, who's coming and your pace, for example one or two stops per half day.

TRAVEL TIME

Real travel times

Ask AI to include real travel times between stops and leave gaps, because traffic always takes longer than you think.

CHECK

Opening hours

AI can get opening hours or ticket prices wrong, so check them on the official website.

Try it now

Your next trip

Give AI the number of days, budget and pace of your next trip, and ask for a relaxed itinerary with travel times.
