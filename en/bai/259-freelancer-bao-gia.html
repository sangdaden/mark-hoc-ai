Freelance designers use AI to quote and lock the scope

Freelance designers use AI to write quotes and lock the scope, so clients stop asking for endless revisions.

Watch videoVideo in Vietnamese

Endless revisions

Freelance designers get endless revision requests because the scope wasn't written down when they took the job.

SCOPE

Spell everything out

Tell a chatbot, an AI chat assistant, the client's request and ask it to list deliverables, number of revisions and deadline.

QUOTE

Three packages to choose from

Ask AI to split the quote into three packages: basic, standard, full. You set the prices based on your effort.

OUT OF SCOPE

Extra work, extra fee

When the client asks for more, ask AI to draft a polite reply saying it's outside the agreement and costs extra.

Try it now

A scope sheet

Ask a chatbot to draft a scope sheet for a logo design job, then send it to the client to confirm.
