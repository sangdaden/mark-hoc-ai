How to get AI to write in your own voice

How to get AI to write in your voice: every sentence AI writes is smooth, but people who know you can tell it isn't you.

Watch videoVideo in Vietnamese

Good, but not you

AI writes smooth sentences, but people who know you can tell it's not your voice.

THREE PIECES

Give it three things you wrote

Choose three things you wrote yourself, like a long message, a post or a letter, and paste them together.

DESCRIBE

Ask AI to describe your style

Ask AI to describe that style: long or short sentences, favorite words, how you address people.

REUSE

Paste the description every time

Fix the description, save it, and paste it every time you ask AI to write. It will sound closer to you.

Try it now

Five lines about your style

Paste three things you've written, ask a chatbot to sum up your style in five bullet points, then save them.
