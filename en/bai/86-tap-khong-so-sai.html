The first time is the real thing

Your first interview, your first difficult customer, your first day teaching. Many things you only learn by doing them for real, and mistakes cost you.

FLIGHT SIMULATOR

Pilots practice first

Pilots and doctors don't learn that way. They practice in flight simulators and on mannequins, facing every bad situation without anyone getting hurt.

SIMULATION-BASED LEARNING

Learning through simulation

This is called simulation-based learning. You act, see the consequences right away, then try again until it feels natural.

WHY IT STICKS

You decide, and know right away

It works because you have to make the decisions yourself, and you find out right away if you were right. Ten pages of theory can't match handling it yourself once.
