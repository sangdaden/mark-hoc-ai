How to run AI offline on your own computer

Need AI to summarize internal files, but your company forbids sending documents outside? Bit suggests running an open model right on your computer.

Watch videoVideo in Vietnamese

Files that can't leave

Mark needs AI to summarize internal files, but his company forbids sending documents to outside services.

OFFLINE

A model on your own machine

Bit suggests: run an open model right on your computer. Everything is processed locally, it works with the internet off, and your data never leaves the machine.

TOOLS

Install it like an app

There are now apps like Ollama or LM Studio that install like normal software: pick a model, download it, and chat.

WHAT YOU NEED

What your computer needs

A laptop with 16 GB of RAM runs a model of about 8 billion parameters, quantized to 4 bits, quite well. A good GPU makes it faster.

LIMITS

The trade-off

In return, a small local model is weaker than the biggest cloud models, and slower. It's good for simple jobs like summarizing, translating and drafting.

Try it now

Try a small model

If your computer has 16 GB of RAM or more, install an app that runs models, download a small model, turn off the internet and ask it a question.
