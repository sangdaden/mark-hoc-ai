Grocery stores use AI to order stock, track credit, arrange shelves

Corner grocery stores use AI to order stock, track credit and arrange shelves: no more running out of bestsellers or overstocking slow items.

Watch videoVideo in Vietnamese

Everything in the owner's head

Corner stores run out of bestsellers and overstock slow items, because everything lives only in the owner's head.

ORDERING

An order list

Copy last week's sales into a chatbot, an AI chat assistant, and ask it to make an order list.

CREDIT

A tidy credit book

Ask AI for a credit book template: date, item, amount, payback date. Use nicknames only; don't paste customers' phone numbers.

SHELVES

What goes where

Describe your items and shop size and ask AI how to arrange shelves: popular items at eye level, heavy items low.

Try it now

A top-ten table

Ask a chatbot for a table of your ten bestsellers with a stock column to count each evening.
