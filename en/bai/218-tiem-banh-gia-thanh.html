Bakeries using AI to work out costs and organize pre-orders

Bakeries use AI to work out costs: many sell lots and still lose money at the end of the month because they never calculated the real cost of each item.

Watch videoVideo in Vietnamese

Busy, but still losing money

Many bakeries sell a lot and still lose money at month's end, because they never fully costed each item.

COST

Cost per item

Type one batch's recipe and ingredient prices into a chatbot and ask for the cost of each item.

PRICE

A few profit levels

Ask AI for a price table at a few profit margins, then pick the one that suits your local customers.

ORDERS

Turn orders into a table

Paste order messages, with phone numbers removed, and ask AI to put them in a table by delivery date.

Try it now

Cost of your best seller

Ask a chatbot to calculate the cost of your best-selling cake, then check the math with a calculator.
