Letting AI help answer customer messages in your online shop

Dozens of messages pile up, there's no time to answer, and customers wait too long and leave. How AI helps Mark.

Watch videoVideo in Vietnamese

Messages piling up

After the livestream, dozens of customer messages pour in. Mark can't keep up, and customers wait too long and leave.

TEMPLATES

A set of ready-made replies

Bit suggests: ask AI to write ready-made replies for common questions, like price, size, shipping fees and returns, then save them as quick replies.

TOUGH CUSTOMERS

Hard messages: let AI draft

When a customer is upset, paste the message into AI and ask for a polite draft with an apology and a concrete fix. Edit it before you send.

CHATBOT

Automatic chatbot replies

If you use a chatbot to reply automatically, let it answer only simple questions, and pass anything about money, returns or complaints to a real person.

CAREFUL

Don't promise what the shop doesn't do

Careful: AI may promise things the shop doesn't offer, like 60-day returns. Always put your real policy in the prompt and check before sending.

Try it now

Five ready-made replies

List the five questions customers asked most this week, ask AI for friendly sample answers, edit them and save them as quick replies.
