Video #236 · Delegating to AI agents · Part 4/5

AI agents that click on the web: watch them and know when to stop

AI agents that use a browser can click and type on the web by themselves. Don't hand it off and walk away: watch what it does.

Watch videoVideo in Vietnamese

Hand it off and walk away

A browser agent can click and type on the web by itself. Don't hand it off and walk away; sit and watch it work.

WATCH

Follow every step

This kind of agent usually shows the screen it sees and logs every click, keystroke and page it opens.

STOP

Stop before key buttons

Tell it to stop before any pay, send or confirm button, so you make the call.

BLOCK

You hold the stop button

If it clicks around wildly, stop it right away. Type passwords and OTP codes yourself; never give them to the agent.

Try it now

Mark the stopping points

Ask a chatbot to list the steps for finding a bus ticket online, then mark which steps must stop to ask you.
