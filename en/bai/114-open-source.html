What are open-source and open-weight AI? How they differ

Heard there are open-source AI models you can download for free. Is it true? Bit explains.

Follow on YouTubeComing soonVideo in Vietnamese

Free AI models?

Mark hears there are open-source AI models you can download for free, and wants to know if that's true.

CLOSED MODELS

Only through a window

Bit explains: many models are closed. You only use them through an app or an API, and you can't see inside.

OPEN-WEIGHT

Download the whole brain

Open models let you download the full set of weights, the trained brain. For example Llama, Qwen, Mistral and DeepSeek.

THE RIGHT WORD

Open-source or open-weight?

To be precise, most are open-weight: you get the brain, but not the data or how it was trained. Fully open-source models share those too.

PROS AND CONS

Run it on your own computer

The upside: run it on your own computer, and your data never leaves it. The trade-off: you need a powerful computer with a good GPU, and it's usually not as good as the biggest models.

REMEMBER

When you see this word

When you see this word, think: a model you can download. Read the license carefully before using it for business.

Try it now

Do it today

Find the page of an open model you've heard of, open its license section, and see whether it can be used for business.
