Write apology and thank-you letters to customers with AI

Write apology and thank-you letters to customers with AI: a generic apology only makes customers more annoyed.

Watch videoVideo in Vietnamese

A generic apology

Apologies like "sorry for the inconvenience" annoy customers more. They need to see you understand what happened.

APOLOGY

Three parts of an apology

An apology letter has three parts: name the exact mistake, how you're fixing it now, and how you'll prevent it.

THANKS

Thanks with details

When thanking regulars, mention a real detail, like what they usually order, instead of a generic thank-you.

SHOP VOICE

Keep your shop's voice

Paste a few old messages so the chatbot learns your voice, then ask for a formal and a friendly version. Remove customers' names and phone numbers.

Try it now

A three-part apology

Describe a time your shop delivered late and ask a chatbot for an apology letter with all three parts.
