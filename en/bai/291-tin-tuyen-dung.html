Write a clear job ad with AI

How to write a clear job ad with AI for a small shop: a vague ad gets everyone applying while the right people scroll past.

Watch videoVideo in Vietnamese

A vague job ad

An ad that only says "looking for someone quick" gets everyone applying while the right people scroll past. A clear ad saves time.

FIVE ITEMS

Five must-haves

A good ad has five things: daily tasks, hours, pay range, address and how to apply.

FAIRNESS

Skills, not age

Don't limit age, gender or hometown. List the skills needed instead, like basic drink-making or using a cash register.

ASK AI

Let AI review the ad

After drafting, ask a chatbot to flag vague parts and anything that could be discriminatory, then shorten it to post.

Try it now

An eight-line job ad

Describe the job your shop needs filled and ask a chatbot for a job ad with all five items, under eight lines.
