Write your weekly report with AI in 10 minutes

Write your weekly report fast with AI: no more spending all Friday afternoon trying to remember what you did.

Watch videoVideo in Vietnamese

A weekly report takes all afternoon

A weekly report eats the whole afternoon usually because you're trying to remember, not because writing is hard.

QUICK NOTES

Three lines each afternoon

Every afternoon, jot three lines in your phone notes: what's done, what's in progress, and where you're stuck.

ASK AI

Fit the company template

At the end of the week, remove client names and confidential numbers, paste your notes into a chatbot, an AI chat assistant, and ask it to fit the company's report template.

CHECK

You own the numbers

AI may round numbers or add work you didn't do. Reread every line and correct it before sending it to your boss.

Try it now

Three lines today

Write three lines about today's work and ask AI to turn them into a short report paragraph.
