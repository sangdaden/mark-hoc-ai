Video #123 · AI for online sellers · Part 1/6

How to use AI to write product descriptions for your online shop

A one-line product description like "pretty, good price" makes nobody stop to read. Mark asks AI to rewrite his silk scarf description in a minute.

Watch videoVideo in Vietnamese

A bland description

Mark opens a shop selling silk scarves. The product description is one line, pretty silk scarf, good price, and nobody stops to read it.

GIVE IT ALL

Give AI the real details

Bit reminds him: AI doesn't know your product. Give it the real details: material, size, colors, how to wash it, and who usually buys it.

PROMPT

A sample prompt

Sample prompt: you're a sales copywriter, the customer is an office worker. Write a headline, three benefits, then the specs, in under 120 words. That's role and output format from the series Getting results from AI.

KEYWORDS

Add the words shoppers search

Add to the prompt the keywords customers type when they search, like silk scarf for the office, so your product is easier to find.

CHECK

Read every word again

Then read every word again. AI likes to add nice-sounding features the product doesn't have, like UV protection, and a wrong claim costs you trust.

Try it now

Rewrite your best seller

Pick your best-selling product, give AI all the real details, ask it to rewrite the description, then edit it in your own voice.
