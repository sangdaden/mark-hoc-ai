A shopping math problem

Mark asks: buy three get one free, each box is $12, I need ten boxes, how much do I pay? AI answers right away: $90. Wrong.

CHAIN OF THOUGHT

Tell AI to think step by step

Add one sentence: think step by step. This is called chain of thought. AI writes out: two rounds of buy three get one gives eight boxes for the price of six. Two more boxes. Pay for eight boxes in total, $96.

WHY

Writing it out gives room to think

Because AI thinks with the very words it writes. Each step written out is more room to work things out, as in video 26 about reasoning.

NEWER MODELS

Thinking mode is built in

Many newer models have a built-in thinking mode. With those, turning it on is enough, no extra instructions needed.

WHEN

Use it for multi-step tasks

Use it for tasks with many steps: working out costs, comparing options, making plans. For simple things like translating one sentence, you don't need it. It just slows things down.

CHECKING

Read each step to catch mistakes

A bonus: you can read every step, so you see right away where AI went wrong, instead of just getting one number.

Try it now

Ask twice, compare

Take a shopping math problem of your own and ask it twice, once normally and once with "think step by step" added, then compare.
