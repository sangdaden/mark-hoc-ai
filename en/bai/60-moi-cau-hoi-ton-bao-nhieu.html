Video #60 · How AI products are made · Part 5/7

How much does each AI question cost? Why free plans have limits

Why do free AI app plans always have limits? Because every question costs real money.

Watch videoVideo in Vietnamese

A thousand more users

For a normal app, a thousand more users costs almost nothing extra. AI apps are different: every question costs money.

WHY

Every token costs money

Because for every answer, the server has to run a giant model, working out each token. The longer the question and the more documents attached, the more it costs.

THE BILL

The bill goes to the product maker

Product makers pay the model company per million tokens. A large model can cost dozens of times, sometimes a hundred times, more than a small one.

FREE PLANS

Why free plans have limits

That's why free plans often limit messages per day, or only allow small models. Monthly plans are priced for the average user.

HEAVY USERS

Some people ask a thousand questions

Some people ask a few questions a day, others run thousands. Heavy users can make a product lose money, so many "unlimited" plans still come with fair-use rules.

TIP

A product maker's trick

A product maker's trick: give easy tasks to a small model, and only call a large model for hard ones. For questions that come up a lot, save the answer so you don't have to work it out again.

Try it now

Check the pricing page

Open the pricing page of an AI app you use. Where is the free plan limited? Now you know why it's limited exactly there.
