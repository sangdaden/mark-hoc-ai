Kids asking why nonstop: let AI explain at their age

Your child keeps asking why? By the tenth time, parents are stuck. AI helps you explain at the right age level, short and simple.

Watch videoVideo in Vietnamese

The tenth why

Your child asks why the sky is blue, then why again, and by the tenth time you're stuck. AI helps you answer at their level.

RIGHT AGE

Say how old they are

Tell AI your child's age and ask it to explain in two or three sentences, comparing to familiar things at home.

CHAIN

Guess the next question

Ask AI to guess three follow-up whys with short answers, so you're not caught off guard.

DOUBLE-CHECK

Health questions go to the doctor

AI can still be wrong; for your child's health, ask the doctor. Looking it up in books together is a good lesson too.

Try it now

Your latest why

Take your child's latest why question and ask a chatbot to answer it for a five-year-old in three sentences.
