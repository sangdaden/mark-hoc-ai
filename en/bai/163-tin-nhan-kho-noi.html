Day 1: a hard message

7-day AI challenge, day one of seven: write a message that's hard to send, like a polite no or a reminder to pay you back.

THREE TONES

Ask AI for three tones

Explain the situation to a chatbot, an AI assistant you chat with, and ask for three versions: gentle, direct and funny.

NO NAMES

No real names needed

You don't need the person's real name. Just describe your relationship and what you want to say.

EDIT

Pick one, then edit it

Choose the version that fits best and change a few words so it sounds like you, because the other person knows how you talk.

Try it now

Day 1 task

Pick a message you've been putting off, do the three steps above in ten minutes, then send it.
