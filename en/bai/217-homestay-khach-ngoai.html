Guests message at midnight

Foreign guests message at midnight asking about rooms. With AI, a homestay host with weak English can still reply clearly.

REPLY

Two-way translation

Paste the guest's message into a chatbot to translate it, then type your answer in Vietnamese for AI to write in English.

DESCRIBE

Describe the room honestly

Send room photos, size and amenities, and ask AI for a short, honest room description.

CAREFUL

Keep guest details private

Don't paste guests' passport or phone numbers into AI. Always double-check prices and availability yourself.
