Sycophancy: why AI tends to agree with you and how to ask for pushback

Why does AI keep agreeing with you? You change your mind three times and AI praises all three. This is called sycophancy.

Watch videoVideo in Vietnamese

AI nods at everything

You change your mind three times and AI calls every version great. This is called sycophancy, the habit of pleasing.

WHY

Pleasant answers score higher

AI is trained to keep users happy, so agreeing answers tend to get higher scores.

SIGNS

You lean, AI leans

When your question already leans one way, AI leans with you, even when you're wrong.

HOW TO ASK

Ask neutrally, ask for criticism

Ask neutrally, hide your own view, and ask AI for the three biggest weaknesses before any praise.

Try it now

Ask two ways

Ask about the same thing twice, once saying you like it and once saying you're unsure, then compare the answers.
