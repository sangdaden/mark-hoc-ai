Where should you start learning AI? A 7-region AI map for beginners

The channel is close to 100 videos, so which one should a newcomer watch first? Mark asks, and Bit opens the map: the whole channel has just 7 regions.

Follow on YouTubeComing soonVideo in Vietnamese

Which video should I watch first?

This is Mark, and Bit the robot. Mark wonders: the channel is close to a hundred videos, so which one should a newcomer watch first? Bit answers: let's open the map. The whole channel has just seven regions.

REGION 1

Getting started

Region one, getting started: AI is really guessing words, why it makes things up, why it forgets. If you're brand new, start here.

REGION 2

Using AI every day

Region two, using AI every day: writing prompts, building your own assistant, and the Claude toolbox.

REGIONS 3 · 4

Inside AI, and measuring it

Region three, inside AI: tokens, attention, and how AI learns from its own mistakes. Region four: how AI gets graded, and its senses for listening, talking and seeing.

REGIONS 5 · 6

Systems and work

Region five, the systems behind it: tool calling, MCP, multi-agent, guardrails, monitoring. Region six, AI and work: which jobs change, and how AI products are made.

REGION 7

Learning with AI

Region seven, learning with AI: a private tutor, learning through simulation, and interactive learning where AI asks you questions back.

WHICH WAY

No need to go in order

You don't need to watch in number order. Pick the region you need most, watch part one of that series, then keep going.

Try it now

Find your region

Write down one thing you want AI to help with this week, then find the region on the map that covers it. The full list is in the pinned comment.
