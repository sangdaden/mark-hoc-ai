Using AI to write livestream sales scripts and short videos

Ten minutes of rambling on a livestream, and viewers come and go. Mark asks AI to plan the script first.

Watch videoVideo in Vietnamese

A rambling livestream

Mark tries a livestream to sell scarves, rambles for ten minutes, and viewers keep coming in and leaving.

OUTLINE

Ask AI for an outline

Bit suggests: ask AI for an outline first. A hook to grab attention, the product, the offer, answers to common questions, then closing the sale.

FIRST 3 SECONDS

An opening that keeps people

For short videos, the first three seconds decide whether people scroll past. Ask AI for five opening lines, then pick the one that fits you best.

QUESTIONS

Prepare your answers

Ask AI to list ten questions customers often ask about the product, with short answers. When a tough question comes up on air, you're ready.

YOUR VOICE

Say it your own way

Don't read the script word for word. Customers buy because they like you, so rewrite it the way you naturally talk.

Try it now

An outline for next time

Before your next livestream, ask AI for a five-part outline and five opening lines, print them and stick them next to your phone.
