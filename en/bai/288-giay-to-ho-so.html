Make a document checklist for visa or school applications with AI

Make a document checklist for visa or school applications with AI: one missing paper can mean waiting another month.

Watch videoVideo in Vietnamese

One missing paper, wait again

A visa or school application missing one paper can mean waiting another month. AI helps you make a checklist.

SOURCE

Start from the official site

Open the embassy's or school's official page, copy the document requirements and paste them into a chatbot.

CHECKLIST

Turn it into a checklist

Ask the chatbot to make a checklist: document name, original or copy, translation needed or not, deadline.

WHO DECIDES

Who has the final say

The embassy or school decides; call them about anything unclear. Don't paste your passport number into a chatbot.

Try it now

Tick off what you have

Paste the document requirements from the official site, ask a chatbot for a checklist, then tick off what you have.
