A headline that keeps you up

Headlines saying AI will replace this or that job cause panic, but read closely and the article usually says something smaller.

CHECK THE SOURCE

Where the prediction comes from

See whether the prediction comes from research with data, or from a company selling AI products.

SPLIT THE JOB

A job is many tasks

A job is made of many small tasks. Ask a chatbot to split yours into tasks: which ones AI can help with and which need a person.

ACT

Practice instead of panicking

Instead of panicking, pick one task AI can help with and practice using it every week. People who know how to use AI usually have an edge.

Try it now

Ten tasks in your job

Ask AI to list ten tasks in your job and mark which ones need a human.
