What is LMArena? Ranking AI with blind matches and Elo scores

No test needed: let real users grade AI.

Watch videoVideo in Vietnamese

Let the users grade it

Last time, we saw AI tests can leak. So let's drop the test and let the users be the judges.

BLIND MATCH

Two anonymous answers

You type a question and get two answers from two unnamed AIs. After reading, you click the better one. Only then are the names shown.

ELO SCORE

Chess-style scores

Each vote is like a chess game: the winner gains points, the loser loses some. Beating a strong opponent earns more. This is called an Elo score, like chess player rankings.

LEADERBOARD

Millions of votes

Add up millions of votes like this, and you get a leaderboard. The best-known site is LMArena, and anyone can try it.

PROS

Close to real life

The good part: the questions are real questions from real users, new every day, so it's very hard to cram for.

CONS

Long and pretty isn't always right

The downside: voters tend to prefer long, nicely formatted, confident answers. A short answer that's correct can sometimes lose.

Try it now

Run your own blind match

You can do this yourself: ask two AIs the same question, copy both answers, and hide the names. Ask a coworker to pick the better one. Do a few questions, and you'll know which AI suits your work.
