Online romance scams: how to spot them

Online romance scams: a sweet stranger chats for weeks, then suddenly has an emergency and asks for money.

Watch videoVideo in Vietnamese

Sweet talk, then money

A stranger online chats sweetly for weeks, then suddenly has an emergency and needs you to send money.

RED FLAGS

Never meeting in person

The common signs: getting close very fast, always avoiding meeting, then asking for money or pitching an investment.

ASK AI

Reread the chat

Remove names and phone numbers, paste the chat into a chatbot, an AI chat assistant, and ask it to point out emotionally manipulative lines.

IF IT HAPPENED

Don't be ashamed

If you've already sent money, don't be ashamed. Call your bank, report it to the police, and tell your family.

Try it now

Share with your family

Ask a chatbot to list five common money requests in romance scams, then share them in your family group.
