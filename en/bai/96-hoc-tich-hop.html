Real life isn't split into subjects

Math class for math, geography class for geography, English class for English. But in real life, what task needs just one subject?

THE TRIP

One trip, four subjects

Planning a trip to Da Lat takes math to work out the cost, geography to choose the route, English if you meet foreign visitors, and teamwork skills.

INTEGRATED LEARNING

Integrated learning

That's integrated learning: learning several subjects at once through a real project. Many schools call it project-based learning.

WHY

Knowing what it's for

Knowledge tied to a real task is easier to remember, and you see right away what you're learning it for.

AI DESIGNS IT

Breaking a project into steps

The hardest part is designing a good project. AI is great at this: it suggests a project, breaks it into steps, and notes which subject each step teaches.

MANY ROLES

AI as the teacher for each subject

As you work, AI plays the teacher for each subject: sometimes checking your cost math, sometimes fixing your English, sometimes asking why you chose that route.

RECAP

The whole series in one picture

The whole series: doing beats watching, turning videos and lectures into exercises, letting AI ask you back, and integrated learning through projects.
