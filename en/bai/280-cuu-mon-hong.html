Rescue salty, burnt or mushy dishes with AI

Rescue dishes that are too salty, burnt rice or mushy food with AI: oversalting doesn't always mean throwing it out; many dishes can be saved while still on the stove.

Watch videoVideo in Vietnamese

Too much salt

Oversalting doesn't always mean throwing it out; many dishes can be saved if you fix them while still on the stove.

DESCRIBE

Say exactly what happened

Tell a chatbot what the dish is, what went wrong and what's left in the kitchen. The clearer the description, the better the fix.

FIXES

Salty, burnt, mushy

For salty soup, add water or vegetables; for burnt rice, scoop out the top part first. AI suggests, you taste bit by bit.

SAFETY

Truly spoiled? Toss it

But food that smells odd, turns slimy or sat out overnight shouldn't be rescued; throw it out to be safe.
