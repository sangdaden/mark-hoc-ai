Why AI text appears bit by bit: latency and streaming

Why does AI text appear bit by bit? It's not for show: that's called streaming. The wait before the first word is called latency.

Watch videoVideo in Vietnamese

Text appears bit by bit

AI text appears bit by bit as if it were typing, and that's not for show. This is called streaming.

STEP BY STEP

Guessing piece by piece

AI predicts one piece of text at a time, so the app sends you each piece as soon as it's ready.

LATENCY

Waiting for the first word

Latency is the delay from pressing send until the first word appears. Heavy files mean a longer wait.

THINKING

Longer wait, better answer

Thinking modes take longer because AI drafts a solution first, but they do better on hard problems.

Stop halfway

When AI goes off topic, hit stop and fix your question instead of waiting for the whole answer.
