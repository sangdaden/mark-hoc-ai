Scale recipes by number of servings with AI

Scale a recipe by number of people with AI: doubling a recipe can still taste off, because salt, water and cooking time don't scale evenly.

Watch videoVideo in Vietnamese

Doubled and it tastes off

Double a recipe and it can still taste off, because salt, water and cooking time don't scale evenly with the ingredients.

CONVERT

Convert by servings

Paste the recipe into a chatbot with the new number of servings, and ask it to recalculate each amount in spoons and bowls for easy measuring.

SEASON SLOWLY

Add seasoning gradually

For salt, sugar and fish sauce, ask AI to list slightly less. Seasoning bit by bit and adding more later is easier.

BIG POT

Watch for doneness

A bigger pot changes the heat and time. Ask AI for the signs a dish is cooked through; don't just watch the clock.

Try it now

Four servings into two

Take a familiar recipe for four people and ask AI to convert it for two.
