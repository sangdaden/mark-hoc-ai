Builders and home repair workers using AI to estimate materials and write clear quotes

Builders and repair workers use AI to estimate materials and lay out clear quotes that homeowners understand at a glance.

Watch videoVideo in Vietnamese

Confusing quotes

A rough handwritten quote can make homeowners suspicious. AI helps workers lay it out more clearly.

ESTIMATE

Estimate materials

Give AI the room size and type of work, ask it to estimate tiles, cement and paint, then compare with your experience.

QUOTE

A quote by item

Ask AI to split the quote into items, materials, labor and number of days, so the homeowner sees where the money goes.

CHECK

Check the numbers and real prices

Material prices vary by area, so ask a nearby shop and check AI's addition with a calculator.

Try it now

A quote for a small job

Take a small job you're working on, ask a chatbot to build a quote by item, then fill in real prices.
