What is an AI agent? When AI does multi-step work for you

Ask AI what to do on a trip, and it gives you a list. But booking tickets, rooms and the schedule is still up to you.

One question, one answer

Ask AI what to do on a trip to the mountains, and it gives you a list. But booking tickets, booking rooms and planning the schedule are still up to you.

What is an AI agent?

An AI agent is AI that doesn't just answer. It's given tools so it can do work for you, over many steps.

STEP 1

The agent splits up the work

You give it a goal. The agent breaks it into steps on its own: find tickets, compare room prices, plan the schedule, add up the cost.

STEP 2

It checks and fixes its own work

If the result isn't good enough, it reviews and fixes it until it meets the goal.

STEP 3

You still make the call

But for important things like paying or sending email, a good agent stops and asks you. Always check before you say yes.

Agents around us

Agents can already do a lot: sort email, make reports. Even the video you're watching was made together with an AI agent.

Try it now

Work together with an agent

Give AI a real goal, ask it to make a step-by-step plan, then work through it together, step by step. That's how you start working with agents.
