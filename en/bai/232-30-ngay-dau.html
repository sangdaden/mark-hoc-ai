Learn fast, don't leak

In your first thirty days, AI helps you learn the job very fast, but pasting company documents by mistake costs you trust.

RULES

Ask in week one

In week one, ask your manager which AI tools the company allows and which data must never leave.

JARGON

A notebook of new words

When you meet unfamiliar jargon, ask AI to explain it with an example. Paste only the word, not internal emails.

HIDE DATA

Rename before you ask

To ask about a real situation, change the client and project names and remove internal numbers.

Try it now

A thirty-day plan

Ask AI for a thirty-day learning plan for your role, split by week, without pasting company documents.
