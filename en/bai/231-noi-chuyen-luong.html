Prepare your salary negotiation with AI

Negotiating salary in an interview: the common mistake is naming a first number that's too low, just from lack of preparation.

Watch videoVideo in Vietnamese

A first number too low

The common mistake in salary talks is naming a first number that's too low, simply because you didn't prepare.

RANGE

Find the going rate yourself

AI doesn't know today's market pay, so look at similar job ads and ask people in the field.

REASONS

Three reasons for your range

Ask AI to list your skills, projects and results, and turn them into three reasons for the range you want.

PRACTICE

Role-play a lowball

Then ask AI to play someone offering low, so you can practice answering calmly while holding your range.

Try it now

Three practice rounds

Ask AI to play the recruiter and practice three rounds of answering the expected-salary question.
