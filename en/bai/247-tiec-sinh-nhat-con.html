Last-minute planning

Your child's birthday is in three days and nothing is ready. AI helps you plan a small party in ten minutes.

DETAILS

Five things to tell it

Give the number of guests, the kids' ages, the budget, the venue and a theme your child loves, like dinosaurs.

PLAN

A day-by-day task list

Ask AI for a task list by day, plus a shopping list and a ninety-minute party schedule.

NOTE

Ask about allergies first

Ask the guests' parents about food allergies first, then tell AI to avoid those foods. Check prices at the shop yourself.

Try it now

Three party games

Ask a chatbot for three games for ten six-year-olds in a small living room.
