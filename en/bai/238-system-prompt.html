What is a system prompt: the hidden instruction inside AI apps

What is a system prompt? The same AI answers briefly in one app and at length in another. The reason is the system prompt, a hidden instruction.

Watch videoVideo in Vietnamese

Every app has its own style

The same AI is brief in one app and wordy in another. The reason is the system prompt.

HIDDEN NOTE

Instructions before you type

A system prompt is a hidden instruction the app gives the AI, read even before your first message.

INSIDE

Role, tone, limits

It usually covers the role, the tone of voice, what it may do and what it must refuse.

YOUR OWN

Your own instructions

Many chatbots let you write your own instructions, like a small system prompt applied to every chat.

Try it now

Three lines up front

Open your chatbot's settings and write three lines about yourself and how you want it to answer.
