How to ask AI about long documents and make it cite page numbers

An 80-page company handbook, and you just want to know how many days off you get? Flipping through takes all morning.

Watch videoVideo in Vietnamese

A document shelf that answers

Last time, each task got its own room with its own document shelf. Now let's turn that shelf into a helper that looks things up for you.

Thick documents, small questions

Your company handbook is eighty pages, and you just want to know how many days off you get. Flipping through it takes all morning.

Ask the document directly

Put the document in a project and ask directly. AI finds the right section, as we saw in the last series, and answers with the page number.

Three places to use it today

It works right away with company handbooks, manuals for things at home, or your kid's school rules.

GOLDEN RULE

Always double-check

The golden rule: make AI give the page number, and open it to check anything important. If it's not in the document, AI must say so.

Try it now

Ask the user manual

Upload the manual for something in your home, ask how to fix an error, and ask for the page number.

Use only this document. What does error E21 mean, and how do I fix it? Give the page number.

What if it worked every week by itself?

Your assistant is good now, but it still waits for you to ask. Part five lets it do weekly work on its own.
