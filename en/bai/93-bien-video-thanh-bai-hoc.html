How to turn YouTube videos into lessons with questions using AI

Saving hundreds of videos

You've saved hundreds of how-to videos. After watching, how much do you remember?

TRANSCRIPT

The transcript

Most videos have a transcript. On YouTube, open the description, choose show transcript, then copy it.

GIVE IT TO AI

Paste it into AI

Paste the transcript into AI. Some apps, like Gemini, can even read a YouTube link directly.

3 THINGS

Ask AI for three things

Then ask AI for three things. One: quiz you on the content, one question at a time. Two: re-explain the hard parts with everyday examples. Three: make flashcards for review.

HALFWAY

Don't wait until the end

Even better, don't wait until you've watched it all. Watch ten minutes, paste that part in, answer a few questions, then keep watching.

DOUBLE-CHECK

AI makes mistakes too

Remember, AI summaries can be wrong too. If something sounds odd, rewind to that part of the video to check.

Try it now

5 questions

Pick a video you just watched, paste its transcript into AI and say: ask me five questions about this video, one at a time, and only ask the next one after I answer.
