A week-by-week moving plan with nothing missed

Plan your move week by week with AI so you don't forget to cancel the internet, transfer utilities or lose things in boxes.

Watch videoVideo in Vietnamese

Forgetting everything

Only after moving do you remember you forgot to cancel the internet. AI makes a weekly plan so nothing slips.

DETAILS

Moving date, people

Tell AI the moving date, how many people and rooms, then ask for a plan split over four weeks.

WEEK BY WEEK

Sort, pack, notify, move

Week one, declutter; week two, pack rarely used things; week three, transfer utilities; final week, pack everyday items.

LABEL BOXES

Number every box

Ask AI for a table of box numbers, contents and destination rooms, so you don't open every box at the new place.
