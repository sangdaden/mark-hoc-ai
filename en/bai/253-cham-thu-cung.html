Pet care with AI: feeding, vaccines, when to see the vet

Care for your dog or cat with AI: set a clear feeding and vaccination schedule and know which signs mean go to the vet now.

Watch videoVideo in Vietnamese

Forgetting vaccinations

You keep forgetting your pet's vaccinations or feed them by feel. AI helps you set a clear schedule.

FEEDING

By age and weight

Tell AI the species, age and weight, and ask for the number of meals and foods it must never eat.

VACCINES

From booklet to calendar

Photograph the vaccination booklet, ask AI to pull out vaccine and deworming dates, then set phone reminders.

VET

Signs to see the vet

Ask AI to list signs that need a vet right away, like not eating, frequent vomiting or trouble breathing. If something seems off, see the vet; don't wait on AI.

Try it now

A one-week schedule

Ask a chatbot for a one-week pet care schedule with feeding times, play times and bath days.
