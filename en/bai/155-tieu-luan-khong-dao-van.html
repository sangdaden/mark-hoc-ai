Using AI to write essays without plagiarism

How do you use AI for an essay without plagiarism? Handing in AI's text word for word is plagiarism, but used well, AI makes your work much better.

AI and plagiarism

Handing in AI's text word for word is plagiarism, but using AI the right way can make your essay much better.

IDEAS

Use AI to argue with you

Use AI to find ideas and debate: ask it to challenge your argument and point out weak spots, not to write it for you.

SOURCES

Check every source yourself

AI often makes up references that sound very real, so for every source you cite, find it and open the original yourself.

DISCLOSE

Say how you used AI

Read your school's rules on AI, and say clearly in your essay what you used AI for, like finding ideas or fixing spelling.

Try it now

Three weak spots

Write the main argument of the essay you're about to hand in, ask AI to point out three weak spots, then rewrite that part in your own words.
