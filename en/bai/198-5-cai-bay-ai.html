Five common traps

The more confident AI sounds, the easier it is to fall into a trap. These are the five most common ones.

TRAPS 1-2

Made-up numbers, fake voices

One: AI makes up numbers and still sounds sure, so check the source. Two: scammers fake a relative's voice, so call back on a number you know.

TRAPS 3-4

Pasting data, trusting detectors

Three: pasting your ID or bank account number into a chatbot. Four: trusting AI-writing detectors, which often guess wrong.

TRAP 5

Letting AI decide for you

Five: letting AI decide about health, money or legal matters. AI helps you understand; qualified people decide.

Try it now

Clean up your chat history

Open your chatbot history, find chats where you pasted personal information, and delete them.
