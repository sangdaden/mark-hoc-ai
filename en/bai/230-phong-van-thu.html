Practice job interviews with AI playing the recruiter

Practice job interviews with AI: many beginners answer vaguely just because they've never practiced out loud.

Watch videoVideo in Vietnamese

Vague answers

Many beginners answer interview questions very vaguely, simply because they've never practiced saying them out loud.

ROLE-PLAY

Give AI the role

Ask AI to play the recruiter for the exact role you're applying for, asking one question per turn.

STORY

Tell a real situation

When you answer, tell a real situation: the context, what you did and the result. Turn on voice mode to practice speaking.

FEEDBACK

Score every answer

At the end, ask AI to score each answer, point out what's still vague, and suggest what you could ask the recruiter back.

Try it now

Fifteen minutes of practice

Practice five interview questions with AI for fifteen minutes and note your weakest answer.
