Translate menus and signs with your phone camera when traveling abroad

Translate menus and signs with your phone camera when traveling abroad: point your camera, and AI translates and explains each dish.

Watch videoVideo in Vietnamese

A menu you can't read

Faced with a menu in a language you can't read? Point your camera at it and AI will translate and explain each dish.

CLEAR PHOTO

Straight and well lit

Take a straight, well-lit photo and ask AI what each dish is made of, whether it's spicy, and the price in your currency.

ALLERGIES

Don't rely on the translation alone

If you're allergic to peanuts or seafood, don't rely on the translation alone. Ask AI to write a sentence in the local language to show the staff.

SIGNS

Reading signs

Same for signs at stations or parking lots: take a photo to find the exit, the timetable or what's not allowed.

Try it now

A package at home

Photograph a package with foreign text at home, ask a chatbot to translate it, then compare with what you already know.
