What is a decision model? Jev and OpenAI's Decisions API

You only need AI to answer with one word, but it writes a whole paragraph: slow and costly.

Ask for one word, get an essay

You only need AI to answer with one word, but it writes a whole paragraph. That's slow, and it costs money.

Software only needs one choice

Lots of tasks only need AI to pick one of a few ready-made answers. Refund, exchange, or price question?

Decision models

That's a decision model. You give it a question and a list of answers, and it picks just one, with how sure it is.

SEP 15, 2026

Jev leads the way

In mid-September 2026, a company called TypeSafe launched Jev, a model that does only this job, very fast and very cheap.

SEP 29, 2026

OpenAI joins in

Two weeks later, OpenAI introduced the Decisions API. OpenAI says it picks an answer in about 0.15 seconds, almost ten times faster than a normal reply.

Why does it matter?

AI agents have to make hundreds of small decisions every day. When each decision is faster, the whole system gets faster and cheaper.

Try it now

Make AI pick just one

When you need AI to sort something, give it a list of answers and tell it to reply with exactly one. The reply is short and easy to use, just like how decision models work.
