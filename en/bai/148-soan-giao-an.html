Planning lessons with AI

Many teachers spend their evenings planning lessons, and AI can write the draft in a few minutes so teachers keep the most important part.

GIVE IT ALL

Tell AI about your class

Tell AI the subject, grade, lesson length, the lesson's goal, and where your students are strong or weak.

STRUCTURE

Ask for the right lesson structure

Ask AI to follow the familiar structure: warm-up, new knowledge, practice, application, with timing for each part.

CHECK

Check facts and examples

Read every point again, especially numbers and examples, then swap generic examples for things close to your students' lives.

Try it now

Three warm-up activities

Pick next week's lesson, give AI the goal and what your class is like, ask for three warm-up ideas, then choose the one that fits best.
