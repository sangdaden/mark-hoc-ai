Video #62 · How AI products are made · Part 7/7

Build your first AI product without code: a 5-box plan

No coding needed: you can still build a small AI assistant for your own work.

Watch videoVideo in Vietnamese

Now build one yourself

In the last six parts, we looked at AI products from the inside. Now let's build one ourselves, no coding needed.

THE PLAN

A sheet with five boxes

Start with a sheet of paper split into five boxes. Box one: who uses it? Box two: what one job does it help them with?

THE PLAN

The other three boxes

Box three: what private data does AI need to know? Box four: what must AI not do? Box five: how will you measure whether it's good?

EXAMPLE

A bakery's plan

Take a bakery: regular customers use it. It answers questions about cakes and opening hours. The data is the menu with prices. It never offers discounts on its own. And ten sample questions it must get right.

BUILD IT

Build it, no code needed

Then build it right inside an AI app you know: a custom GPT in ChatGPT, a Gem in Gemini, or a Project in Claude. Use boxes two and four as the instructions, then upload the documents from box three.

TEST IT

Try it with real people

Run the ten sample questions from box five. Then let three real people try it, and see what they ask that you never thought of.

RECAP

The whole series on one page

Engine, fuel, steering wheel, brakes, dashboard. Pick the right task, design for mistakes, measure, count the cost, learn from stumbles. That's the whole series, on one sheet of paper.

Try it now

Fill in five boxes, build in 30 minutes

Pick a question customers or coworkers ask you every week. Fill in the five boxes, then build a test version in thirty minutes. Thanks for following the whole series.
