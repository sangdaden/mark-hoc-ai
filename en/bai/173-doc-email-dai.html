Read long emails fast and reply to every point with AI

Read long emails fast and reply to every point with AI: missing one request in a reply is a very common mistake.

Watch videoVideo in Vietnamese

Long email, missed point

Missing one request when replying to a long email is a common mistake. AI can pull out what the sender really needs.

FIND REQUESTS

List the requests

Paste the email into AI and ask it to list each request, each deadline and anything unclear to ask about.

DRAFT

Draft a reply point by point

Then ask AI to draft a reply that covers every point in order, polite and short.

PRIVACY

Remove private details

Before pasting, remove account numbers, phone numbers and customer details, and reread the reply before sending.

Try it now

One long email

Pick a long email, remove personal details, ask AI to list the requests, then compare with the list you made yourself.
