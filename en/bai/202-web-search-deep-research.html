Web search vs Deep Research: what's the difference

What's the difference between web search and Deep Research? When AI answers recent news with links, it has turned on web search and is looking things up online.

Watch videoVideo in Vietnamese

AI looks things up online

When AI answers recent news with links, it has turned on web search: it's looking things up online itself.

QUICK

Web search checks a few pages

Web search quickly checks a few pages and summarizes them, good for the weather or movie times.

DEEP

Deep Research reads deeply

Deep Research reads dozens of sources in a few minutes, then writes a long report with citations.

CHECK SOURCES

Click the links

Even with web search, AI can misread things, so click a few links to see what the sources really say.

Try it now

Turn on search, open two links

Turn on web search in a chatbot, ask about something from this week, then open two of the links it gives to check.
