What the photos you post online reveal

What do the photos you post reveal: one proud photo of your child can reveal their school, class and pickup time.

Watch videoVideo in Vietnamese

A proud parent photo

One proud photo of your child on the first day of school can reveal their school, class and pickup time.

DETAILS

Things that give you away

Watch for house numbers, license plates, name badges, boarding passes, and even the view out the window showing where you live.

ASK AI

A photo checklist

Ask a chatbot, an AI chat assistant, for a checklist of revealing details, then check your photos yourself before posting.

POST LATER

Post after you're home

Post travel photos after you return, because posting right away tells strangers your home is empty.

Try it now

Check your last three photos

Open the last three photos you posted, check them against the list, and blur or remove anything that gives too much away.
