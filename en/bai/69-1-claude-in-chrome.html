Claude clicks and types in Chrome: a real run from start to finish

Install Claude in Chrome, have it read and compare web pages, then let it click and type its way through filling a spreadsheet.

This time for real

In video 69 you only saw Claude in Chrome for a few seconds. This part does it for real in Chrome, from installation to Claude filling in a spreadsheet by itself.

SETUP

Install and connect your account

Go to the Claude in Chrome page on the Chrome Web Store, click Add to Chrome, then click Authorize to connect your Claude account. Note that the extension needs a paid plan, Pro or higher, and only runs on desktop Chrome.

SIDE PANEL

A panel beside the page

Click the Claude icon in the toolbar and a side panel opens on the right. Claude sees the page you have open, right next to the chat.

TASK 1 · READ

Summarize and compare

The first task is reading: open the Wikipedia article on artificial intelligence and a search page about machine learning, then ask Claude to summarize each in three points. It answers right in the side panel, with a comparison of how the two ideas differ in scope, method and type of problem.

TASK 2 · ACT

Claude clicks and types

The second task is acting: ask Claude to open markhocai.com, get the titles of the five newest videos, and fill them into the open Google Sheets file, one video per row. After 17 actions, the five titles are neatly in the sheet.

SMART

It even skips on its own

Better still, two videos on the page weren't marked as released yet, so Claude treated them as not out and skipped them, then told you why.

YOU STAY IN CONTROL

There's always a stop button

While Claude works, Chrome shows a bar saying Claude is controlling the browser, with a Stop Claude button so you can stop it at any time.

SAFETY

Two things you always do yourself

Unfamiliar pages can hide instructions you can't see, so only use Claude on pages you trust. Logging in to your bank and clicking pay are things you always do yourself.

Try it now

Compare three items before buying

Open three tabs for something you're planning to buy and ask Claude to build a table comparing price and warranty. Here it compares three air fryers, and points out that the pages don't show delivery fees yet, so you can ask before deciding.
