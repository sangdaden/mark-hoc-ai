Using AI to summarize customer reviews and find what to fix

Over a hundred reviews, praise and complaints mixed together, and no time to read them all. Mark asks AI to summarize.

Watch videoVideo in Vietnamese

A hundred reviews

Mark's shop has over a hundred reviews, praise and complaints mixed together, and he has no time to read them all to see what needs fixing.

SUMMARIZE

Paste them in, let AI group them

Bit shows him: copy the reviews, hide customers' names, and paste them into AI. Ask it to sort them into praise and complaints, with how many times each point comes up.

PRIORITY

What to fix first

Then ask AI to rank them: which complaint comes up most, and suggest three things to fix first. Mark finds over twenty customers saying the color looks different from the photo, exactly the lesson from part two.

REPLY

Answering bad reviews

For bad reviews, ask AI to draft a public reply that thanks them for the feedback and says clearly what the shop has fixed. Future customers will feel reassured.

NOTE

Never use AI for fake reviews

One thing you should never do: ask AI to write fake reviews. That's cheating customers, and the marketplace can shut down your shop.

Try it now

Three things to fix

Copy your 50 latest reviews, ask AI for the three things customers complain about most, and fix one of them this week.
