Where does your data go when you chat with AI? How to protect it

You pasted a whole payroll sheet into AI to work out bonuses, then panicked: where does this data go?

Watch videoVideo in Vietnamese

A whole payroll sheet

Mark pastes his team's whole payroll sheet into AI to work out bonuses, and only afterwards wonders where this data goes.

WHERE IT GOES

Stored on servers

Bit explains: the conversation is sent to the provider's servers, and is usually saved in your history.

TRAINING

It may be used for training

With personal accounts, many apps can use your chats to train new models unless you turn it off. Settings usually has a switch for this, under privacy or data controls.

DON'T PASTE

Things you shouldn't paste

Never paste passwords, OTP codes, ID numbers, customer data or company secrets into a personal AI account.

HOW TO DO IT

Hide details before pasting

The safe way: hide details before pasting, and replace real names with Employee A, B, C. AI still calculates correctly without knowing who's who. Use temporary chat mode, or a company account that promises not to use data for training.

Try it now

Open Settings now

Open Settings in the AI app you use, find privacy or data controls, and choose whether your data can be used for training.
