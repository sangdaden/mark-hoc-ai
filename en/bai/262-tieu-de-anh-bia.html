Video #262 · Making short videos with AI · Part 3/6

Write video titles and thumbnails without clickbait

Write video titles and make thumbnails with AI: clickable without clickbait, so viewers don't feel tricked.

Watch videoVideo in Vietnamese

Sensational, then gone

Sensational titles get clicks, but viewers who feel tricked scroll away and never come back.

KEYWORDS

Words people actually type

Ask a chatbot, an AI chat assistant, how viewers search for this topic, then put the keywords at the start of the title.

HONEST

Deliver what you promise

Ask AI for ten titles, then cross out any that promise more than the video actually delivers.

THUMBNAIL

Few words, one expression

A thumbnail should have just a few big words and one expressive face. Ask AI to cut the title to under six words.

Try it now

Ten titles

Ask a chatbot for ten titles for your latest video, then pick one that's both clear and honest.
