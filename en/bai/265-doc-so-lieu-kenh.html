Read channel analytics so your next video keeps viewers

Read your channel analytics with AI so your next video keeps viewers longer: high views don't always mean a good video.

Watch videoVideo in Vietnamese

Views aren't enough

High views don't always mean a good video; watch time shows how far the video keeps people.

THREE NUMBERS

Numbers worth watching

Mark học AI looks at three numbers: completion rate, the second viewers leave, and comments with questions.

COMPARE

Keepers and droppers

Paste a few videos' stats into a chatbot, an AI chat assistant, and ask it to compare the ones that held viewers with the ones scrolled past.

ONE AT A TIME

Change just one thing

Ask AI for one change for the next video, then measure again. Change several things at once and you won't know what worked.

Try it now

Your last five videos

Paste the stats of your last five videos into a chatbot and ask which one kept viewers the longest.
