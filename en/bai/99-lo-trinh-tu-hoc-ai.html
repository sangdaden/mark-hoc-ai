A 4-week self-study path for AI beginners

Where do I start?

Mark wants to teach himself AI, but doesn't know where to start. Bit maps out a four-step path, about one week per step.

STEP 1 · WEEK 1

Understand what AI really is

Step one, understand what AI really is: it guesses words, sometimes makes things up, sometimes forgets. Watch the Getting started region on the map.

STEP 2 · WEEK 2

Use it every day

Step two, use it every day: write 4-part prompts, teach AI who you are, save a prompt library.

STEP 3 · WEEK 3

Look inside

Step three, look inside: tokens, attention, AI learning from mistakes. Once you understand the machine, you know when to trust it.

STEP 4 · WEEK 4

Bring it into real work

Step four, bring it into real work: pick a repeated task, hand it to AI, measure the results, and use AI to learn something new.

THE SECRET

Watch one, do one

The secret is one video a day, then ten minutes on the try-it-now part. If you only watch without doing, you'll forget as soon as you close it.

Try it now

Schedule 4 weeks

Open your calendar, block ten minutes every evening for four weeks, and write the name of the step you're on.
