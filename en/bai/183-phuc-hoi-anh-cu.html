Restore old photos with AI without changing your relatives' faces

Restoring old photos with AI is fast, but sometimes grandma's or grandpa's face comes out looking like someone else.

An old photo turns into a stranger

AI restores old photos very quickly, but sometimes your grandparents' faces come out looking like someone else.

GOOD SCAN

Scan or photograph it well

Scan the photo, or take a straight-on picture in even light with no glare.

KEEP THE ORIGINAL

Always keep the original

Store the paper photo safely, keep the original scan separately, and let AI edit only a copy.

MADE-UP FACES

AI can invent faces

Where the photo is blurry or torn, AI guesses and draws in features, so don't call it a real photo or use it to fool anyone.

Try it now

Check with your family

Photograph an old picture, ask a chatbot to sharpen it, then show an older relative and ask if the faces still look right.
