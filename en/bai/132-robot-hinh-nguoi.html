Humanoid robots

Humanoid robots can now fold clothes, pour water and stack boxes, but they still work much more slowly than you.

WHY

Easy for people, hard for robots

Strangely, AI beat humans at chess long ago, yet a robot is still very clumsy at holding a soft shirt.

HOW THEY LEARN

See, understand, then move

New robots use an AI model that sees through a camera, understands an instruction, then controls the hands, learning from thousands of human demonstrations.

TODAY

Where they work

Today robots do real work mostly in warehouses and factories, where tasks repeat and the routes are familiar.

Try it now

Watch robot videos with a sharp eye

When you watch a robot video, look for the small text saying whether it's real speed or sped up, and whether the robot works on its own or someone controls it remotely.
