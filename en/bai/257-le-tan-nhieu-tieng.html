Hotel receptionists use AI to speak many languages

Hotel receptionists use AI to talk with guests in many languages: Korean and Japanese guests can still be served well.

Watch videoVideo in Vietnamese

Three guests, three languages

Korean and Japanese guests stand at the desk together, and a receptionist who only speaks English can still help them with AI.

VOICE

Translate at the desk

Turn on the voice mode of a chatbot, an AI chat assistant; guests speak their own language and AI translates instantly.

PHRASE CARDS

Common phrases ready

Ask AI for greetings, breakfast hours and wifi instructions in several languages, printed as cards at the desk.

CAREFUL

Documents and payments

Don't photograph guests' passports into AI. For room rates and fees, double-check the guest understood correctly.

Try it now

Check-out time in three languages

Ask a chatbot to translate "check-out is at twelve noon" into three languages, with pronunciation.
