What is an AI jailbreak, why it's dangerous and how AI blocks it

Tricking AI into breaking rules

Some people trick AI into breaking its rules to do things it normally refuses. That trick is called a jailbreak.

TRICKS

Role-play and roundabout stories

They often make AI play a role, tell a roundabout story, or split a harmful request into many small steps.

DANGER

Why it's dangerous

The danger is that AI could give harmful instructions, and people who try it can get their accounts banned.

DEFENSES

Many layers of guardrails

AI companies block it in layers: training the model to spot tricks, adding filters, and patching new holes quickly.

Try it now

Ask about AI's limits

Ask a chatbot to list the things it refuses to do, and the reason for each.
