Kids using AI for homework: what should parents do?

Kids using AI for homework: copying the answer finishes the work fast, but they learn nothing, and in a class test without AI they're stuck. How parents turn AI into a tutor.

Watch videoVideo in Vietnamese

Copying answers from AI

Copying answers from AI finishes homework fast, but your child learns nothing new.

THE PROBLEM

Right answers, empty head

Every answer handed in is right, but in a class test without AI, your child doesn't know where to start.

TUTOR

Turn AI into a tutor

Tell AI to act as a tutor: no answers, only step-by-step hints, and questions back so your child thinks for themselves.

PRACTICE

Let AI make more exercises

When they're done, ask AI for three similar problems for your child to solve alone, and wherever they go wrong, AI explains exactly that part again.

Try it now

The tutor instruction

With your child, type this at the start of the chat: I'm in grade 5, don't give me the answer, give me hints step by step.
