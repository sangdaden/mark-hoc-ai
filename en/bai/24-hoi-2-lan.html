Ask twice, get two answers?

Last time, AI learned to answer like an assistant. But ask the same question twice, and you get two different answers. Why?

Scoring a hundred options

Remember: AI guesses the next piece of text. But it doesn't guess just one word. It scores a hundred options. Coffee 40%, bubble tea 25%, and so on.

A prize wheel

Then it draws one based on those odds, like a prize wheel where bigger slices are easier to hit. This time it lands on coffee, next time maybe bubble tea.

The "temperature" knob

This amount of chance has a knob, called temperature. Low temperature: steady, almost the same every time. High temperature: playful and creative, but more likely to drift off topic.

Every word is a new draw

Every word is a new draw. One different word at the start, and the whole rest of the answer goes a different way.

Creative or exact?

For creative work like naming things or writing slogans, hit regenerate three times and pick the best idea. For work that needs to be exact, like math, ask AI to show its steps and check its own work.
