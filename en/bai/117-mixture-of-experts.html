What is Mixture of Experts (MoE)? Why big models can still be fast

New models have huge numbers of parameters but still run fast? The secret is Mixture of Experts (MoE).

Follow on YouTubeComing soonVideo in Vietnamese

Big but still fast?

Mark reads the specs of a Mixture of Experts model: a huge number of parameters, yet it still runs fast. He wants to know why.

MoE

A team of experts

Bit explains: Mixture of Experts, MoE for short, splits a model into many small experts, plus a coordinator called the router.

HOW IT RUNS

Only a few are called each time

For each token that comes in, the router calls only the few best-fitting experts, and the others rest.

THE BENEFIT

A big store, a light load

That way, the model has a huge store of knowledge, but only runs a small part each time, so it stays fast and cheap.

GETTING IT RIGHT

Not split by school subject

Note: the experts aren't neatly split into math, literature or history. They divide the work themselves while learning, in ways people find hard to name.

REMEMBER

When you see this word

When you see this word, think: a big model that only uses a few experts at a time, so it's fast. Many powerful models today work this way.

Try it now

Do it today

Next time you read the specs of a new model, look for "MoE" or "active parameters" (the parameters used each time). Now you know what they mean.
