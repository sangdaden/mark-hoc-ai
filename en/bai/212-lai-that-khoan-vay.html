Ask AI to read loan and credit card terms and work out the real interest

Ask AI to read loan and credit card terms and work out the real interest: ads show a low rate, but with all the fees and a different calculation method, the real cost can be much higher.

Watch videoVideo in Vietnamese

Advertised rate vs real rate

Loan ads show a low rate, but add all the fees and change the calculation method, and the real cost can be much higher.

COVER FIRST

Hide personal details

Cover your name and ID number on the loan papers, then ask a chatbot, an AI assistant you chat with, to explain each line.

METHOD

Original or reducing balance

Ask AI whether interest is charged on the original amount or on the reducing balance, and to list every fee.

CREDIT CARDS

Statement date and due date

For credit cards, ask AI to explain the statement date, the due date, and what happens if you only pay the minimum.

Try it now

Calculate the total

Ask a chatbot to calculate the total you'd repay on a sample loan, then take your questions to the bank.
