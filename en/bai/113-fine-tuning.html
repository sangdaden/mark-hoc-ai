What is fine-tuning?

Mark reads in the news that a company is fine-tuning its own model, and wants to know what fine-tuning means.

MEANING

Extra training for a trained model

Bit explains: fine-tuning is extra training, adjusting a model that has already finished learning with a special set of examples. Like someone who finished high school taking a job training course.

EXAMPLE

A real example

For example: give it thousands of customer service conversations, and the model learns that company's tone and way of handling things.

COMPARISON

Different from prompts and RAG

It's different from a prompt, which gives instructions every time you ask, or RAG, which lets it look things up in documents, as in video 25. Fine-tuning changes the model's own habits.

REMEMBER

When you see this word

When you see this word, think: extra training for the model. Everyday users rarely need it. Good prompts and good documents solve most things.

Try it now

Do it today

Before you think about fine-tuning, try things in order: prompt → RAG. Write a really clear prompt, then give AI your documents to look things up in. Most tasks are done after these two steps.
