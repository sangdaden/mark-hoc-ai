What is a virtual lab? Learning with free PhET simulations

In school, the lesson on electric circuits was just pictures in a book; there was no lab, and you were scared of short circuits.

Follow on YouTubeComing soonVideo in Vietnamese

Only looking at pictures

In school, for the lesson on electric circuits, you only got to look at drawings in the book. You wanted to build one yourself, but there was no lab, and you were scared of short circuits.

VIRTUAL LAB

A virtual lab

Now there are virtual labs. You drag batteries, wires and bulbs onto the screen, press run, and see right away whether the bulb lights up.

PHET

A free library of simulations

A well-known one is PhET from the University of Colorado in the US: over a hundred simulations in physics, chemistry, biology and math, free, in many languages.

CHANGE ANYTHING

Things you can't do in real life

The best part is you can change things you can't change in real life: turn off gravity, zoom into molecules, make time run a hundred times faster.

GUESS FIRST

Guess, then press

But don't just click for fun. The trick is to guess the result before you run it. When you guess wrong is when you learn the most.

ASK AI

Stuck? Ask

If you get stuck, take a screenshot and send it to AI, and ask why the result was different from what you guessed.

Try it now

The pendulum

Go to the PhET site and choose a simulation, for example the pendulum. Guess whether a longer string makes it swing faster or slower, then press run to check.

PART 3

Role-play with AI

For experiments with objects, there are virtual labs. What about practicing conversations with people? Part three: role-play with AI.
