What is prompt injection: hidden text that gives AI orders

What is prompt injection? You ask AI to summarize a web page and it praises some unknown product. That may be prompt injection: hidden commands slipped into the AI.

Watch videoVideo in Vietnamese

A summary that goes off track

You ask AI to summarize a web page and it praises an unknown product. That may be prompt injection.

HOW

White text on a white page

Bad actors hide commands in web pages or documents that people can't see but AI can read.

DANGER

When AI has tools

It's most dangerous when AI can read your email or click on the web, because a hidden command may tell it to send data out.

DEFENSE

Unknown documents are read-only

With unfamiliar documents, tell AI to only summarize and not follow any instructions inside.

Try it now

Spot hidden commands

Paste an unfamiliar text into a chatbot and ask it to point out sentences that look like commands for an AI.
