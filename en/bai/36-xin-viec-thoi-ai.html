How to use AI to improve your resume and practice job interviews

Employers use software, including AI, to filter applications. A resume that doesn't match the job description may be rejected before anyone reads it.

Watch videoVideo in Vietnamese

You have the skills, now apply

Last time, we found the skills that keep their value. Now it's time to show them to employers.

The other side uses AI too

Employers use software more and more, including AI, to filter applications. A resume that doesn't match the job description may be rejected before anyone reads it.

Compare your resume to the job description

Paste the job description and your resume, and ask AI: what's my resume missing for this role? It shows what matches and what isn't mentioned.

Only write what's true

But only rewrite what you've really done, more clearly. Don't let AI add experience you don't have.

Practice interviews with AI

Then ask AI to play the interviewer for that exact role. It asks one question at a time, you answer, and it gives feedback. A few rounds and you'll feel much more confident.

Try it now

Two things to try

Remove sensitive personal details from your resume, paste it with the job description, and ask "what's missing?". Then ask AI to give you a mock interview, one question at a time.

Play the interviewer for this role and ask me one question at a time.

Last part: a 90-day plan

Finally, you need a plan so you don't fall behind. The last part puts everything into a 90-day roadmap.
