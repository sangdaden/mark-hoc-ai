What can AI do on farms and in factories?

A drone finds sick rice

A drone flies over a field, and AI points to the exact patch of rice that's sick.

FARMING

Spray only where needed

AI compares leaf colors across thousands of photos and marks the sick areas, so farmers spray only where needed instead of the whole field.

FACTORIES

Cameras check every product

In factories, cameras with AI check every product on the conveyor belt, finding scratches or crooked caps faster than human eyes.

MACHINES

Listening to machines to predict breakdowns

AI also listens to how machines vibrate and warns before one breaks, so technicians can fix it in time and the whole line doesn't have to stop.

Try it now

Snap a leaf and ask AI

Take a photo of a wilting leaf from a plant at home, send it to a chatbot to ask why, then check with someone experienced at growing plants.
