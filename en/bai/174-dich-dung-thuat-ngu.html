Smooth translation, wrong terms

AI translations read smoothly, but a common problem is each page translating the same term a different way.

GLOSSARY

Give AI a glossary

Give AI your company's glossary, one translation per term, and ask it to use them consistently.

NUMBERS AND NAMES

Check numbers and names

After translating, check numbers, dates, currencies and names, because one wrong number can ruin a contract.

REVIEWER

Important documents

For contracts or legal documents, AI's translation is only a draft. A qualified person must review it.

Try it now

Your ten terms

Make a table of ten terms from your field, paste it with one page of a document, ask AI to translate, then check which words don't match the table.
