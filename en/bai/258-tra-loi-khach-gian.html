Reply to angry customers with AI without overpromising

How to reply to angry customers with AI: understand what they need and write a gentle reply without making promises you can't keep.

Watch videoVideo in Vietnamese

Correct but still angry

An angry customer who gets a robotic reply gets twice as angry, even if you didn't get a single word wrong.

READ THE NEED

What the customer really needs

Remove names and order numbers, paste the message into a chatbot, an AI chat assistant, and ask what the customer needs most.

DRAFT

Three points in a reply

Ask AI for a three-point draft: acknowledge the feelings, say clearly what you'll do, and give a specific time.

CAREFUL

No reckless promises

Always reread before sending. Don't let AI promise refunds or compensation the company hasn't approved.

Soften a dry reply

Ask a chatbot to rewrite a dry reply so it's softer but still follows policy.
