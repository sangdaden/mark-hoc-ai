Which AI model should you choose? Haiku, Sonnet, Opus and Fable compared

One company, a whole lineup of models. Which one should you pick?

▶ Watch videoVideo in Vietnamese

Names that sound like poetry

Haiku, Sonnet, Opus, Fable. Luna, Sol, Astra. One company, a whole lineup of models. Which one should you pick?

Like a scooter, a car and a truck

Think of it like choosing a vehicle. The same company makes scooters, cars and trucks. No vehicle is "the best", only the right one for the job.

SMALL TIER

Fast and cheap

Small models like Anthropic's Haiku or OpenAI's Luna answer fast and cost very little. They suit simple tasks done in large amounts.

MIDDLE TIER

Balanced

Middle models like Sonnet or Sol balance quality, speed and price. They're the default choice for most everyday work.

LARGE TIER

Very powerful

Large models like Opus or Astra reason deeply and handle hard tasks, but they're slower and much more expensive.

TOP TIER

Anthropic's Fable

Fable is the top tier Anthropic added in mid-2026, above even Opus. It's made for very long, very hard work, like letting an AI agent run a whole project for hours. It's also the most expensive tier.

The real price gap

The gap is big. With OpenAI, the largest tier costs about a hundred times more than the smallest for the same amount of text.

Try it now

Start with the middle tier

Start with a middle-tier model. If it's not good enough, move up to a large one. Only very long, hard work needs Fable. For simple, repeated tasks, go down to a small one for speed and low cost.
