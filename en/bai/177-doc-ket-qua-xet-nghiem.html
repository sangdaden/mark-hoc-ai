Read blood test results with AI: understand the terms and know what to ask your doctor

Read blood test results with AI: understand abbreviations like GLU and LDL instead of googling symptoms and worrying.

Watch videoVideo in Vietnamese

A confusing test result

Holding a lab report full of abbreviations, many people google it, guess a disease and worry for nothing.

EXPLAIN

AI explains the terms

AI can explain what each measure, like blood sugar or cholesterol, actually measures, in plain words.

DOCTOR

Your doctor reads the results

AI doesn't diagnose, because the numbers depend on age, medication and medical history. Your doctor reads the results.

QUESTIONS

Bring questions to your appointment

Ask AI to list questions for your doctor, and remember to cover your name and patient ID before taking a photo.

An old test result

Cover the name on an old test result, ask AI to explain the terms and suggest three questions for your doctor.
