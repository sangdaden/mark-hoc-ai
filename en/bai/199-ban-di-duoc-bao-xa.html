Five self-check questions

Watching lots of videos doesn't mean you use AI well. These five questions show how far you've come.

Q 1-2

Writing and fixing prompts

One: you can write a prompt with a role, examples and an answer format. Two: when the result isn't right, you know how to fix the prompt.

Q 3-4

Checking and staying safe

Three: you always double-check numbers AI gives you. Four: you always remove personal information before pasting.

Q 5

One real task every week

Five: every week you give AI at least one real task, at work or when writing.
