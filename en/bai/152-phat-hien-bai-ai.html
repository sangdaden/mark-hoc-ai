Are AI-writing detectors reliable?

Are tools that detect AI-written work reliable? They can wrongly flag a student who wrote their own work, and miss AI text with a few words changed.

Watch videoVideo in Vietnamese

Detectors that get it wrong

Tools that detect AI-written work can wrongly flag a student who wrote their own essay, and miss an AI essay with a few words changed.

WHY

It's only guessing probabilities

These tools only guess probabilities from sentence patterns, so neat, grammatically correct writing can easily look AI-written.

CHANGE IT UP

Change how you set assignments

Instead of hunting for AI work, change how you set assignments: tie them to students' own experiences, things that happened in class, or local stories.

PROCESS

Grade the whole process

Grade the whole process, including the outline, the draft, and a few short oral questions about the student's own essay.

Try it now

Change next week's assignment

Rewrite one assignment for next week so students must describe their own real experience, and add an outline due a day earlier.
