AI investing apps that promise profits: 5 signs of a scam

AI investing apps that promise steady daily profits are almost certainly scams. 5 common warning signs:

Watch videoVideo in Vietnamese

Promised steady profits

An app calling itself an AI investor and promising steady daily profits is almost certainly a scam.

WARNING SIGNS

Five common signs

Common signs: a fixed promised return, pressure to deposit fast, fees to withdraw, and a private chat group.

THE TRUTH

Nobody can predict prices for sure

Nobody, AI included, can predict prices for sure. A promise of never losing money is exactly the trap.

ASK AI

Let AI check the offer

Paste the offer into a chatbot, with personal details removed, and ask for the warning signs. If you already paid, contact your bank and the police.
