How do chatbots learn to answer and say no? From parrot to assistant

After reading a whole library, AI is still just a "parrot": ask how to sleep better, and it might continue with a list of other questions.

Watch videoVideo in Vietnamese

A parrot that only continues

Last time, AI read a whole library. But at that point it's like a parrot: it can only continue sentences, not answer you.

You ask one thing, it says another

Ask it how to sleep better, and it might continue with a list of other questions, because online, questions often sit next to each other like that.

Learning from sample conversations

Next, people write lots of sample conversations: if asked this, answer like that. AI copies them, and starts answering the actual question.

People give scores

Then AI writes a few different answers, and people score which is better. Helpful, polite, honest answers get rewarded. Made-up or dangerous answers lose points.

Why AI knows how to say no

This is also when AI learns to refuse harmful requests, and learns the polite tone you see every day.

Try it now

Teach AI about you

Go to the personalization settings and write three lines: who you are, whether you want long or short answers, and how to talk to you. Give good answers a thumbs up, bad ones a thumbs down, and say why.

I'm a middle school teacher. Keep answers short, with examples. Use a friendly, informal tone.
