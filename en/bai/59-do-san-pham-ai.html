How to measure AI product quality: sample questions and A/B tests

A leaderboard tells you which model is best, but not whether your product is good.

Watch videoVideo in Vietnamese

Leaderboards aren't enough

A leaderboard says this model is the best. But whether your product is good, a leaderboard can't tell you.

WAY 1

A set of sample questions

The first way: a set of sample questions, taken from real customer questions, with the right answers. A bakery might have fifty: cake prices, opening hours, how to order.

WAY 1

Rerun it every time you change something

Every time you change something, the model or the instructions, rerun the whole set. Any question that used to be right and is now wrong shows up right away, before customers see it.

WAY 2

Signals from real users

The second way: listen to real users. Not just the like and dislike buttons. If they ask the same thing again, they weren't satisfied. If they edit the answer before using it, it was close. If they leave halfway, something's wrong.

WAY 3

A/B testing

The third way: an A/B test. Half the customers see the old version, half see the new one. Whichever gets more cake orders wins.

RECAP

Three ways together

All three together: the question set keeps quality steady, real signals show where it hurts, and A/B tests help you choose a direction.

Try it now

Click the button, add a reason

Next time you see like and dislike buttons in an AI app, click one, and add a line saying why. That's how you help improve the product.
