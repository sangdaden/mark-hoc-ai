Teaching kids to double-check when AI gets it wrong

AI can be wrong and still sound very sure, and children tend to believe it right away. Three habits that help kids check AI.

Watch videoVideo in Vietnamese

AI is wrong, but confident

AI can say something wrong in a very confident voice, and children tend to believe it right away.

ASK FOR SOURCES

Ask where it got that

Teach your child the first habit: ask AI where this information comes from, then open that source to see if it's real.

COMPARE

Compare with the textbook

The second habit: compare with the textbook or ask the teacher, and note down anything that differs to ask about.

GAME

A spot-the-mistake game

Turn it into a game: every evening, find one mistake AI makes together, and whoever catches it gets a point.

Try it now

Catch a mistake tonight

Tonight, ask AI a question about where you live, like a local specialty dish or a school's name, then check together whether it's right.
