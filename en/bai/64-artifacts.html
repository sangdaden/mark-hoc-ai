Copy, paste, fix all afternoon

You ask Claude for a planning table, then spend all afternoon copying, pasting and fixing the formatting. There's a neater way: Artifacts.

ARTIFACT

It opens right next to the chat

An artifact is a finished piece that opens right next to the chat: a document, slides, a small spreadsheet, or even a tool you can click.

Edit with words

To change it, just say: add a notes column, make it blue. Or click in and edit it directly, like a text document.

Download it or share a link

When you're done, download it as Word, PDF or PowerPoint, or send a link for others to see. Artifacts are private by default, and you choose who can see them.

Free or paid?

The free plan can already create, edit and share. Ready-made templates like Docs, Slides and Design need a paid plan.
