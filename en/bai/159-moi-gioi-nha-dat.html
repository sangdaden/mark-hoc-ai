Writing property listings

Every home needs its own listing, and agents often spend a whole evening just writing them.

LISTINGS

Write from real specs

Give AI the floor area, number of rooms, which way the house faces, and the distance to schools and markets, and ask for a clear listing with no exaggeration.

PHOTOS

Edit photos, don't mislead

AI can brighten photos and remove clutter, but don't add windows or gardens that don't exist, or buyers who come to see it will lose trust.

COMPARE

A comparison table for buyers

Ask AI for a table comparing a few homes for a buyer, like price per square meter, commute time, pros and cons, then check every number yourself.

Try it now

A 150-word listing

Take a home you're selling, give AI the real specs, ask for a listing under 150 words, then check every detail.
