AI voice-cloning scams: how to help grandparents stay safe

AI voice scams: crooks clone a grandchild's voice and call grandparents asking for money urgently. Two ways to protect the whole family.

Watch videoVideo in Vietnamese

Faking a grandchild's voice

Scammers can now use AI to fake a grandchild's voice and call grandparents asking for money urgently.

VOICE CLONE

A few seconds of voice is enough

Just a few seconds of voice taken from a video posted online is enough for AI to copy a voice that sounds very real.

PASSWORD

Set a family password

Pick a family password phrase together, and if a call asks for money urgently but can't say the phrase, hang up.

CALL BACK

Hang up and call back

Then call back the grandchild's usual number yourself, because scammers can't answer the family member's real phone.

Try it now

Set the password today

Call your grandparents today, choose an easy-to-remember password phrase together, and agree to send money only after calling back.
