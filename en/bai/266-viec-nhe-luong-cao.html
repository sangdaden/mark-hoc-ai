Easy-job, high-pay scams: how to spot them

Online easy-job, high-pay scams: the signs to spot before you lose money.

Watch videoVideo in Vietnamese

An offer too good to be true

An ad for a work-from-home job, a few hours a day with high pay, is often the first step of a scam.

RED FLAGS

Three common signs

The signs: the work is too easy and the pay too high, hiring happens in chat groups, and you must pay a fee or deposit first.

THE TRAP

Small rewards, then they keep the big money

Scammers pay small rewards a few times so you trust them, then make you deposit large sums for tasks and keep it all.

IF IT HAPPENED

Call your bank and the police

If you've sent money, call your bank right away, report it to the police, and don't send more to get it back.
