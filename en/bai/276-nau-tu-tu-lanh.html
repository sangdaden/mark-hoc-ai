What to cook today? Let AI suggest dishes from your fridge

What to cook today? Ask AI for dishes from what's in your fridge, when it's full but you only know a few old recipes.

Watch videoVideo in Vietnamese

A full fridge, no ideas

The fridge is full and you still don't know what to cook, because you only know a few old dishes.

LIST

Name everything you have

Photograph the fridge or type a list of what's left, spices included, and send it to a chatbot, an AI chat assistant.

ASK AI

Three dishes, little shopping

Ask for three dishes using only what you have, noting what you'd need to buy and how long each takes.

SAFETY

Spoiled food goes out

AI can't smell food. Anything that smells odd, has changed color or is past its date goes in the trash, no regrets.

Try it now

Five things for dinner

Open the fridge, list five things left and ask AI for a dinner idea for tonight.
