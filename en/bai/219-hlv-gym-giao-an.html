Gym trainers writing workout plans with AI

Twenty clients

A gym trainer has twenty clients, each with a different goal. AI helps write a plan for each one.

PLAN

Four weeks, step by step

Enter the goal, number of sessions and equipment into a chatbot and ask for a four-week plan.

TRACKING

Notes after every session

After each session, write down weights, reps and how it felt. At the end of the week, ask AI to sum up progress so you can adjust.

SAFETY

Medical conditions need a doctor

Use client codes instead of real names. Anyone with heart problems or injuries should check with a doctor first.

Try it now

A weekly summary message

Ask a chatbot to write a weekly summary message for one client, with one thing to praise and one to work on.
