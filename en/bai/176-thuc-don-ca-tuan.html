What's for dinner?

The hardest question every afternoon is what to cook. AI can plan the whole week's menu in one go.

THE DETAILS

Tell AI about your household

Tell AI how many people eat, your weekly budget, what anyone avoids and how much time you have to cook each night.

SHOPPING

One combined list

Ask AI to combine the week's ingredients into one list grouped by meat and fish, vegetables and dry goods.

BALANCE

Balanced meals

Ask AI to balance vegetables, protein and carbs. If someone has a condition like diabetes, ask a dietitian.
