Write polite complaint letters that get results with AI

Write polite complaint letters that get results with AI: the angrier the letter, the easier it is to ignore.

Watch videoVideo in Vietnamese

Too angry to work

The angrier a complaint letter is, the easier it is to ignore. Letters that work are calm, clear about what happened and clear about what you want.

FACTS

State what happened

Open with the facts: what you bought, when, the order number, what's wrong, with photos as evidence.

WHAT YOU WANT

Say what you want

Then say clearly whether you want an exchange, a refund or a free repair, with a reasonable deadline.

ASK AI

Tone the letter down

Draft it while angry if you like, then ask a chatbot to remove the harsh words. Delete your phone number and address before pasting.

Try it now

A five-sentence letter

Describe a faulty product and ask a chatbot for a five-sentence complaint with the facts and what you want.
