Same question, different results

You and a friend ask AI for the same thing. Why does their answer come out great and yours doesn't? The difference is the instruction you type, called a prompt.

What is a prompt?

A prompt is the message you send to AI. Think of AI as a smart assistant who just met you. It doesn't know who you are or what you need yet.

A short prompt makes AI guess

If you only type "write an ad", AI has to guess: what are you selling, to whom, how long should it be? Guessing gives you something generic.

FORMULA

The 4-part formula

The fix is simple: include all four parts. Role: who AI should act as. Context: who you are and who you're writing for. Task: exactly what you need done. Format: how it should look and how long it should be.

EXAMPLE

Put it into a real prompt

For example: You are an ad copywriter. I sell salted coffee, and my customers are college students. Write a Facebook post introducing our new drink, under 80 words, in a fun tone.

TIP

Not happy? Just keep chatting

Not quite right? Just send another message: shorter, more fun, add the price. AI will fix it right away.

Remember all 4 parts

Next time you ask AI something, remember all four parts. Role, context, task, format.

You are ____. I am ____, writing for ____. Please ____. Format it as ____.
