Day 6: learning a new skill

Day six of seven: no time to learn something new? Fifteen minutes a day with AI is enough to start.

START SMALL

Pick a tiny skill

Choose a really small skill, like cooking three vegetarian dishes, and tell AI your current level.

SEVEN SESSIONS

A plan of seven mini sessions

Ask AI to split it into seven 15-minute sessions, each with something to do, not just something to read.

CHECK

Let AI test you at the end

In the last session, ask AI for five questions and have it grade you. Review whatever you got wrong.

Try it now

Day 6 task

Ask a chatbot for a seven-session plan for the skill you chose, and do the first session tonight.
