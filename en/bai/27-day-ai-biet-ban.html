Your own assistant

You now understand how AI works. This six-part series turns it into your own assistant. Part one: teach AI who you are.

Introducing yourself every time

Every time you open a new chat, AI forgets who you are. You have to start over: what you do for work, what kind of answers you want.

A note stuck on the desk

Chat apps have a place called custom instructions. It's like a note stuck on the assistant's desk: every time you start, AI reads it first.

Before and after

Same question, but with this note the answer is shorter, closer to your work, and uses the tone you like.

What to write there

Just three things: who you are, whether you want long or short answers, and how you'd like AI to talk to you. Don't put passwords or account numbers here.

Try it now

Three lines about you

Go to settings, find personalization, write three lines about yourself and save. Open a new chat and try a question you often ask.
