Make presentation slides fast with AI from an outline

Make presentation slides fast with AI from an outline you already have, and avoid stuffing a whole paragraph onto one slide.

Watch videoVideo in Vietnamese

Slides full of text

A common slide mistake is stuffing a whole paragraph onto one page. AI can help you split it up from your outline.

ONE IDEA

One idea per slide

Give AI your outline and how long you'll talk, and ask for a layout with one main idea per slide and titles that state the conclusion.

REAL NUMBERS

Fill in real numbers

Numbers in AI's draft are just placeholders. Put in your real figures and note the source.

CUT WORDS

Cut the text

Then cut the text hard. Keep a few keywords per slide and say the explanation out loud.

Try it now

Your next presentation

Ask AI to turn the outline of your next presentation into at most ten slides, then fill in the real numbers yourself.
