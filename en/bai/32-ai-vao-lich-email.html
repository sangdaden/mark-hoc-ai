Should you let AI into your calendar and email? 3 levels of safe access

How far should you open the door?

The last part of the series: chat apps can now connect to your calendar, email and files. The question is how far to open the door and still stay safe.

What is a connection?

Connecting means you let AI open a door into your account. The permission screen spells out what it's allowed to do.

LEVEL 1

Let it read

Level one: let it read. AI looks at your calendar and reads your email to answer your questions. This is the safe level to start with.

LEVEL 2

Let it draft

Level two: let it draft. AI writes emails or appointments for you, but you read them and click send yourself.

LEVEL 3

Don't let it act alone yet

Level three, sending, deleting or paying on your behalf: not yet. One misunderstanding, and the email is gone or the data is lost.

Try it now

Ask about your calendar, review permissions

Connect your calendar and ask when you're free this week. Then go to your Google account's security settings, see which apps have access, and remove the ones you don't use.
