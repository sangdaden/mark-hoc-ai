Make posters and invitations with AI without garbled Vietnamese text

AI makes beautiful posters and invitations, but Vietnamese text inside the image often has wrong accents or warped letters.

Watch videoVideo in Vietnamese

A nice poster with garbled text

AI makes beautiful event posters, but Vietnamese text in the image often has wrong accent marks or warped letters.

LAYOUT

Let AI sketch a layout

Tell AI the type of event, date, time, place and main color, and ask for a few layout ideas.

LEAVE ROOM

Make a background, leave space for text

When making the image, tell AI not to write any text and to leave an empty area, so you can add the words later.

TYPE IT

Type the text yourself

Open a photo app on your phone, type the event name, date and address yourself, and check every accent mark before printing.

Try it now

A birthday card with your own text

Ask a chatbot for a birthday card background with no text, then type your own message on it with a photo app.
