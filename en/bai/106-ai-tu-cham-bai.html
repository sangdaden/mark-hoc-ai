What is self-critique? How to get AI to grade and fix its own work

AI wrote your time-off email, and it's okay but something's missing? Have it grade its own work.

Follow on YouTubeComing soonVideo in Vietnamese

The first draft

AI writes Mark an email asking for time off. It's okay, but Mark feels something is still missing.

SELF-CRITIQUE

Have AI grade itself

Bit suggests: have AI grade its own work, called self-critique. Write: point out three weaknesses in this email, then write a better version.

THE RESULT

The second draft is better

AI spots them itself: no return date, no mention of who covers the work, and the tone is a bit abrupt. The second draft fixes all three.

CRITERIA

Give it a measuring stick

The grading works better if you give it a measuring stick: clear, polite, brief, each scored out of ten.

SWITCH ROLES

Read it like the recipient

Even stronger: have AI play the recipient, like your boss, and give feedback. That combines this with part two.

LIMITS

Self-grading isn't fact-checking

But self-grading doesn't replace fact-checking. If AI made up a number, it may still praise it as correct, as in video 8 about hallucination.

Try it now

Add one grading round

For your next important piece of writing, add one round: point out three weaknesses, then rewrite. Compare the two versions.
