Ask AI to write a bedtime story starring your child

Ask AI to write a bedtime story with your child's name: kids listen more closely when the hero has their name, and AI writes it in a minute.

Watch videoVideo in Vietnamese

Your child is the hero

Kids listen more closely when the main character has their name. AI writes that story in a minute.

RECIPE

Name, age, lesson

Tell AI your child's nickname, age, favorite animal and one lesson, like waiting your turn.

JUST RIGHT

Short, gentle, calm ending

Ask for about three minutes of reading, short sentences, no scary scenes, ending with a peaceful bedtime.

PRIVACY

Nicknames only

Use only the nickname; don't give the full name, school or address. Skim the story before telling it.

Try it now

Tonight's story

Ask a chatbot for tonight's story, with your child and their favorite animal, about brushing teeth.
