Motorbike mechanics use AI to explain repairs and quotes

Motorbike mechanics use AI to explain faults and quotes clearly, so customers don't suspect they're being upsold.

Watch videoVideo in Vietnamese

Customers don't understand

Customers often suspect mechanics of inventing problems, just because they hear part names without understanding what's broken.

EXPLAIN

Plain words for customers

Type the bike's condition into a chatbot, an AI chat assistant, and ask it to explain in plain words for the customer.

QUOTE

Itemize every cost

Ask AI to split the quote clearly: parts, labor, what must be done now and what can wait.

CAREFUL

The mechanic decides, AI writes

AI only helps write it clearly. The diagnosis and price are still decided by the mechanic checking the bike.

A sample quote message
