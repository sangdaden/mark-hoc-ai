4 rules for using AI safely with kids at home

Every home has rules for phones, but very few have rules for AI. Four simple rules for the whole family.

Watch videoVideo in Vietnamese

House rules for AI

Every home has rules for phones, but very few homes have rules for AI.

RULE 1

Keep private info out

Rule one: don't give AI full names, your home address, photos of friends, or anyone's passwords.

RULE 2

AI helps, it doesn't do it for you

Rule two: AI helps explain and gives ideas, but homework, birthday wishes and important messages you write yourself.

RULES 3 AND 4

Check, and tell an adult

Rule three: check before you believe. Rule four: if AI says something strange or makes your child uncomfortable, tell an adult right away.

Try it now

Stick the rules on the fridge

As a family, write these four rules on a sheet of paper, stick it on the fridge, and agree to look at them again in a month.
