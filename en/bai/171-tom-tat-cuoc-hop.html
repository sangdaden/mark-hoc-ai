Summarize a meeting with AI from a recording or notes: who does what, by when

Summarize meetings with AI from a recording or notes: after an hour-long meeting nobody remembers who's doing what, so let AI write tidy minutes.

Watch videoVideo in Vietnamese

The meeting ends, the tasks are forgotten

After an hour-long meeting nobody remembers who's doing what. AI can turn a recording or your notes into tidy minutes.

ASK FIRST

Ask everyone before recording

Ask everyone's permission before recording, and don't record meetings with confidential topics.

THREE PARTS

Decisions, owners, deadlines

Ask AI to summarize in three parts: decisions made, who does what, and the deadline for each task.

DOUBLE-CHECK

Check names and dates

Double-check names and dates, because AI often mishears names, then send it to the team to confirm.

Try it now

Your last meeting notes

Take the notes from your last meeting, remove anything sensitive, and ask AI to split them into decisions, owners and deadlines.
