Letting AI help write feedback on students' work

Asking AI to help write feedback: forty essays, each needing its own comment, is when AI helps the most.

Watch videoVideo in Vietnamese

Forty comments

You've graded forty essays, and each needs its own comment. That's when AI helps the most.

RUBRIC

Give AI your rubric

Give AI your grading rubric and the grade you gave, and ask it to write a comment with one strength, one thing to fix, and one specific suggestion.

GRADES

Grades are still yours

The teacher still decides the grade. AI only helps make the comment clear and warm, without repeating the same template.

PRIVACY

Remove students' names

Before pasting work into AI, remove students' full names and personal details, and use an ID number instead.

Try it now

Five graded essays

Pick five graded essays, remove the names, ask AI for comments in the three-part format, then rewrite them in your own voice.
