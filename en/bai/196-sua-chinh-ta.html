Spell-checking that erases your voice

Ask AI to check spelling and many people get back a brand-new text: correct, but no longer in their voice.

MARK ONLY

Only mark the mistakes

Tell AI to only mark spelling mistakes and clumsy sentences, with a reason, and never rewrite whole paragraphs.

YOU DECIDE

You decide each change

Go through each mistake AI found. Fix the real ones, and keep regional expressions that are just how you talk.

CAREFUL

AI makes mistakes too

AI can also get tone marks or names wrong, so check a dictionary when in doubt.

Try it now

A three-column table

Paste something you just wrote, ask a chatbot for a three-column table of original sentence, problem and suggestion, then fix it yourself.
