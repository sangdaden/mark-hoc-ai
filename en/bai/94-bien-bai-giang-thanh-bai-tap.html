60 pages of slides

Your teacher sends a sixty-page slide file before the exam. You read it from start to finish, then read it again.

FROM PART 1

Rereading loses to answering

As part one said, rereading doesn't help you remember as well as answering questions yourself. So turn your materials into exercises.

NOTEBOOKLM

Only uses your materials

One tool that does this very well is Google's NotebookLM. You upload slides, PDFs and notes, and it only answers based on exactly those materials.

ONE CLICK

All kinds of exercises

One click gives you quizzes, flashcards, mind maps, or an audio summary to listen to on the go.

EXPLANATIONS

Where you went wrong, where to look

Get a quiz question wrong, click explain, and it shows which part of the materials has the right answer.

CLAUDE, CHATGPT

Any app can do it

Claude or ChatGPT can do it too: upload the file and ask for questions. The trick is to also ask why each wrong answer is wrong.

Try it now

10 multiple-choice questions

Upload a lecture file and say: make ten multiple-choice questions from this material, ask them one at a time, and if I pick the wrong answer, explain why it's wrong.
