A 90-day roadmap

The last part. We put all three series into a 90-day roadmap that takes just 15 minutes a day.

MONTH 1

Understand AI

Month 1: understand AI. Rewatch the "Inside AI's brain" series and try two tips each week, like putting your request first, turning on web search for news, and using thinking mode for hard questions.

MONTH 2

Build your own assistant

Month 2: build your own assistant. Write custom instructions, make a prompt library, create a project for your main task, and set up a scheduled task.

MONTH 3

Bring AI into your work

Month 3: bring AI into your work. Pick three repeated weekly tasks to hand to AI, practice being AI's boss, and update your resume with new skills.

Track your progress

Each week, write down how much time AI saved you. Watching that number grow keeps you going.

Try it now

Start today

Save this roadmap, and pick the smallest task to do today. Start small, but start now.
