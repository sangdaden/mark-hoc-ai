How to make AI answer in tables, checklists or JSON (output format)

Asked AI to compare 3 phone plans and got 5 long paragraphs, and still don't know which to pick? Say exactly how you want the answer.

Follow on YouTubeComing soonVideo in Vietnamese

Tired eyes

Mark asks AI to compare three phone plans and gets back five long paragraphs. After reading, he still doesn't know which plan to pick.

OUTPUT FORMAT

Say exactly how to answer

Bit says: specify the output format. Add: answer in a table, with columns for plan name, price, data and who it suits. One table, and you can choose at a glance.

FORMATS

A format for every task

Each task has its format: a table to compare, a checklist for to-dos, three bullet points for a summary, an email with a subject line to send.

LENGTH

Set a limit

Add a length limit: under a hundred words, or readable in thirty seconds. AI will cut the extra parts on its own.

JSON

When it goes to another program

If the result goes on into a spreadsheet or another app, ask for JSON or CSV format, so you can paste it right in.

TIP

Give it a blank template

The strongest trick: paste a blank template for AI to fill in, like the sample examples in part one.

Try it now

Compare with a table

Next time you need to compare two things you're thinking of buying, ask AI to answer in a table, plus one line saying which to choose.
