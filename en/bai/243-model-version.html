Why AI changes after an update: model versions

Why does AI change its style after an update? Yesterday it answered one way, today another, for the exact same question. The company may have changed the model version.

AI changed its style

Yesterday AI answered one way, today another, for the exact same question. The company may have just changed the model version.

VERSION

Each version, a different brain

A model version is a version of the AI's brain. A new one has learned more, so its tone and approach may change.

CHECK THE NAME

The model name in the app

Many apps show the model name at the top of the chat, and some let you pick the old version for a while.

STAY STEADY

Save your go-to prompts

Save the prompts you use often with sample results; when a new version comes out, rerun them to see what needs fixing.

Try it now

Note the model name

Find out which model your chatbot is running and write its name with today's date in your notes.
