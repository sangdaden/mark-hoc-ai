How to save prompts you use often: build your own prompt library

Retyping the same long prompts every day? And the best prompt you ever wrote is lost in your chat history.

Watch videoVideo in Vietnamese

Typing it again every day

Last time, AI learned who you are. But every day you still retype the same long prompts.

Great prompts get lost

You once wrote a great prompt, and AI did exactly what you wanted. A few days later, it's buried in your chat history and you can't find it.

Turn your prompt into a template

Turn it into a template: keep the good part, and leave blanks for the parts that change. The 4-part formula from an earlier video makes a great template.

A shelf of templates

Make a few templates for the tasks you do most. When you need one, grab it, fill in the blanks, paste it in, and you're done.

Shortcuts for speed

Even faster: save templates as text shortcuts on your phone. Type a few characters, and the whole prompt appears.

Try it now

A "My prompts" page

Open your notes app, create a page called "My prompts", and save the three prompts you use most, with blanks for the parts that change.

Summarize [paste email] in [number] points, and list what I need to do and the deadline

Chats full of mixed tasks

Your prompts are tidy now, but your chats are a mix of everything. Part three gives each task its own room.
