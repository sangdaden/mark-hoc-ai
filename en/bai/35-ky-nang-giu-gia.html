The work that still needs you

Last time, we found the parts of your work that still need you. Now let's name the skills inside them.

SKILL 1

Judgment

First is judgment. AI gives you ten options, but which one to pick, and when, is still up to you.

SKILL 2

Fact-checking

Second is fact-checking. AI can make things up, as we know. People who can spot the mistakes will always be valuable.

SKILL 3

Connecting with people

Third is working with people: listening, persuading, comforting and leading a team. Customers still want to talk to a person.

NEW SKILL

Being AI's boss

And a new skill: being AI's boss. Give clear tasks, check the results, ask for fixes, and only then use them.

Try it now

Grade AI's work with 3 questions

Give AI a real task from your week. Then grade its work with three questions: is it right, is it complete, does it fit the person receiving it?

You have the skills. How do you show them to employers? Part four shows how.
