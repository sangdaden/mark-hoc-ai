What shouldn't you paste into AI? How to get help without exposing real data

Asking AI to write a customer email, and pasting a whole table of names, phone numbers and account numbers? Wait a moment.

Watch videoVideo in Vietnamese

Pasting your whole customer file?

You ask AI to write an email to a customer, and you paste a whole table of names, phone numbers and account numbers. Wait a moment.

Where what you type goes

What you type is sent to the AI company's servers and is often stored. Depending on the service and your settings, it may also be used to train AI.

DON'T PASTE

What not to give AI

Don't paste passwords, one-time codes (OTP), card numbers, ID numbers, health records, customer data or confidential company documents.

HOW TO

You can still ask, just hide the details

You can still get help from AI. Just replace real details with placeholders: a name becomes "Customer A", an amount becomes "X".

HOW TO

Fill in the real details afterwards

Once AI writes the draft, you fill the real details back in yourself. Same result, and your real data never leaves your device.

Check your privacy settings

Also, open the privacy settings of the AI app you use, see what it stores, and turn off what you don't want. If your company has its own AI rules, follow them.

Try it now

Ten seconds before you paste

Next time you ask AI for help with real information, take ten seconds to change names to "Customer A" and amounts to "X" before you paste.
