Ask AI to plan an emergency fund and 6 months of saving

Ask AI to help you build an emergency fund and a 6-month savings plan: without one, a single illness or job loss means borrowing.

Watch videoVideo in Vietnamese

No emergency fund yet

Without an emergency fund, one illness or job loss means borrowing. AI can help you plan six months of saving.

ESSENTIALS

List your fixed costs

List your essential monthly costs: rent, food, utilities, transport. Write amounts only, no account numbers.

GOAL

Split it by month

Ask AI to add it up, set a fund target of a few months of essentials, then split it by month.

REALISTIC

Adjust if it's too much

If it's too much, ask AI to find costs to cut or stretch the plan. Ask a professional where to keep the money.

Try it now

A six-month savings table

Ask a chatbot for a six-month savings table, then set a reminder to transfer money on payday.
