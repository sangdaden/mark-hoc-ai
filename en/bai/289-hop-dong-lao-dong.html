Use AI to understand an employment contract before signing

Ask AI to help you understand an employment contract before signing, so you don't find the unfavorable terms only when trouble starts.

Watch videoVideo in Vietnamese

Sign first, read later

Many people sign an employment contract and read it later, only finding the unfavorable terms when trouble starts.

HIDE NAMES

Cover personal details

Before pasting into a chatbot, delete your name, ID number, the company name and the exact salary.

FOUR SECTIONS

Four sections to check

Ask AI to explain four sections simply: probation, hours and overtime, days off, and termination terms.

QUESTIONS

Bring your questions

AI helps you understand and draft questions. Ask HR about anything unclear, and a lawyer about major terms.

Try it now

Three questions to ask

Paste a section of a contract with names removed and ask a chatbot for three questions to ask before signing.
