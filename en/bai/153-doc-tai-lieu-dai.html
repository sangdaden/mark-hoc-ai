Using AI to read long documents and still understand them

Asking AI to summarize a long reading is the fastest way, but not the best: skim a one-page summary and you forget it. How Mark reads 50 pages with AI.

Watch videoVideo in Vietnamese

Fifty pages of reading

The lecturer assigns fifty pages before class, and asking AI to summarize is the fastest way, but not the best.

MAP

Ask for a map first

First ask AI to map the document: the main sections, the main idea of each, and which part matters most for your course.

READ DEEPLY

Read the key part yourself

Then read the important part carefully yourself, and when something is hard, paste that passage into AI and ask for a simpler example.

SELF-CHECK

Let AI quiz you

When you're done, ask AI to ask you five questions about the document. If you can answer, you've really understood it, not just skimmed a summary.

Try it now

Map, read, get quizzed

With this week's course reading, ask AI for a map, read one part carefully, then let AI ask you five questions.
