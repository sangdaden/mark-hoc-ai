Learn English vocabulary in context with AI so it sticks

Memorized, then forgotten

Memorize fifty new words and you'll forget most of them a week later. Learning in context with AI sticks longer.

CONTEXT

Words that fit your life

Give AI a word and ask for three sentences about your own life, like work, cooking or football.

PHRASES

Learn whole phrases

Ask AI for the phrases the word usually comes in, because native speakers talk in chunks, not word by word.

REVIEW

Write sentences for AI to check

The next day, write your own sentences with the word and ask AI to correct them and explain briefly.

Try it now

Today's three words

Pick three English words you saw today and ask a chatbot to make sentences about your own job.
