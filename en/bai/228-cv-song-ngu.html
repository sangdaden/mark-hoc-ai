One CV for every job

Sending the same CV to every job is the mistake that gets applications rejected within seconds.

READ THE AD

Pull out the keywords

Paste the job ad into a chatbot and ask it to pull out the five most mentioned skills.

MATCH

Tailor the CV to the role

Paste your CV with your phone number and home address removed, and ask AI which experience to move to the top.

BILINGUAL

A natural English version

Ask AI to write the English version the way a native speaker would, then check every number and don't let AI add work you didn't do.
