A 15-minute home workout with no equipment: even busy people can ask AI for a session that fits them.

Fifteen minutes at home

No gym or equipment needed: AI can still plan a 15-minute home workout for you.

LIMITS

Explain your limits

Tell AI your age, how active you are and old injuries like knee pain, so it avoids exercises that don't suit you.

START EASY

Start really easy

Ask AI to split the session into warm-up, main workout and stretching, very easy in the first week, then build up.

STOP

Stop if it hurts

If you feel pain, dizziness or shortness of breath, stop right away and see a doctor.

Try it now

Tonight's workout

Tell AI when you're free and where it hurts, ask for a 15-minute living-room workout, and try it tonight.
