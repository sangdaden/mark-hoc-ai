Learning games for kids from things already at home

Toys from bottle caps

No need to buy toys: bottle caps and paper boxes at home are enough for learning games. AI comes up with how to play.

LIST

Name the items, name the goal

List five things you have, your child's age, and what you want them to learn, like counting, colors or letters.

RULES

Ten minutes, clear steps

Ask AI for three games, ten minutes each, with clear steps and a way to make them harder.

SAFETY

Watch out for small items

For kids under three, remind AI to avoid small items that can be swallowed, and always play alongside them.

Try it now

This afternoon's game

Look around the kitchen, pick three items, and ask a chatbot for a counting game to play this afternoon.
