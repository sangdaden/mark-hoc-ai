A one-month posting calendar that never runs dry

Plan a month of video posts with AI so you never run out of ideas or wake up wondering what to post today.

Quitting for lack of ideas

Many small channels quit after a few weeks, not from laziness, but from wondering every morning what to post.

SERIES

One big idea, many parts

Mark học AI works in series of five or six parts, so one big topic covers a whole week.

MONTHLY PLAN

A thirty-day table

Give your list of ideas to a chatbot, an AI chat assistant, and ask for a thirty-day schedule mixing easy and hard topics.

BACKUP

A stash of spare ideas

Ask AI for a list of backup ideas too, so on busy days you still have something to post without rushing.

Try it now

Four series for next month

Tell a chatbot your channel's topic and ask it to split it into four series for the next four weeks.
