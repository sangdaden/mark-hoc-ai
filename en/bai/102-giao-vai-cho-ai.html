What is a system prompt? Give AI a role and set the rules

Ask AI something and get an answer that's correct but generic, written for anyone? Bit shows two tricks.

Follow on YouTubeComing soonVideo in Vietnamese

A generic answer

Mark asks AI for a workout plan and gets back a long text that's correct but generic, written for anyone.

ROLE

Give AI a role

Bit shows the first trick: give it a role. Write: you are a coach for beginners who only have twenty minutes a day. The role helps AI pick the right knowledge and the right way to talk.

A SPECIFIC ROLE

The clearer the role, the better

The more specific the role, the better. "Expert" is vague. "Coach for office workers with back pain" is much clearer.

SYSTEM PROMPT

Rules for the whole conversation

The second trick: a system prompt, instructions that sit above the whole conversation. For example: answer in five lines at most, and ask me if information is missing. Every question after that, AI follows these rules.

WHERE

You have a system prompt too

You don't need to be a programmer to have a system prompt. The custom instructions in Settings, or a Project's instructions, are exactly that, as in videos 27 and 66.

NOTE

A role doesn't replace knowledge

Note: giving AI the role of a doctor doesn't make it a doctor. For important matters about health or money, still ask a real person.

Try it now

Write 3 rules for AI

Write three rules you want AI to always follow, like be brief, give examples, use a friendly tone, then paste them into custom instructions.
