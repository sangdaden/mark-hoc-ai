Praise and give feedback to staff to keep them

Praise and give feedback to keep your staff: employees tend to quit when the owner only speaks up about mistakes.

Watch videoVideo in Vietnamese

Speaking up only about mistakes

Staff tend to quit when the owner only speaks up about mistakes. Timely praise and well-delivered feedback help keep people.

PRAISE

Be specific

Praise specifically, like "you arranged the shelf so neatly that customers find things faster", instead of just "great job".

FEEDBACK

In private, about the work

Give feedback in private, about the work rather than the person, then pick one thing to fix together.

ASK AI

Rehearse what to say

Tell a chatbot the situation, no real names needed, and ask for three gentle ways to say it.

Try it now

One compliment today

Ask a chatbot for a two-sentence compliment on something good an employee did, then say it in person.
