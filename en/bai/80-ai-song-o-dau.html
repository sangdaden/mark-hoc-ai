Where does AI run? Cloud, data centers, APIs and on-device AI

You type a question on your phone, but the AI isn't inside your phone. It lives very far away.

AI is very far away

You type a question on your phone, but the AI isn't inside your phone. It's very far away.

DATA CENTER

AI's building

Big AI models run in data centers: buildings full of server racks with GPUs, a special kind of chip. They use as much power as a whole neighborhood, and need constant cooling.

API

A door called an API

Apps send your question over the internet to that building, through a door called an API, and get the answer back. Most AI apps don't run their own models. They rent them through this door, as video 56 explained.

REGION

Which country your data is in

Which region the data center is in, meaning which country, matters too. Banks and hospitals often have to choose where their data lives because of regulations.

PEAK HOURS

Overloaded, try again later

At peak hours, millions of people ask at once. The system turns on more machines by itself, called autoscaling. If it can't keep up, you see the familiar message: overloaded, try again later.

ON-DEVICE

AI right on your phone

There are also small models that run right on your phone, called on-device AI. No internet needed, your data never leaves the device, but they're less smart. Many new phones use both: light tasks on the phone, hard ones sent to the cloud.

Try it now

Check the status page

Next time AI is slow or shows an error, don't ask ten times. Search the app's name plus the word status, open the company's status page, and see if there's an outage.
