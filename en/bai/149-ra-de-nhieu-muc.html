Tests at three levels

From one lesson, AI can write easy, medium and hard question sets, with answers, in just a few minutes.

LEVELS

Name the levels clearly

Name the levels clearly, recall, understanding and application, and how many questions per level, so the test is balanced instead of all easy questions.

ANSWERS

Make AI explain the answers

Ask AI to explain why the right answer is right and where the wrong options go wrong, because a clumsy explanation often points to a flawed question.

SOLVE IT YOURSELF

Solve it yourself first

Before handing out the test, solve each question yourself, because AI can still give wrong answers, especially for multi-step math problems.

Try it now

Ten multiple-choice questions

Ask AI for ten multiple-choice questions on the lesson you just taught, four recall, four understanding, two application, with worked answers.
