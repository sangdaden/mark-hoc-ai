Phone repair shops using AI to explain faults and quotes clearly

Phone repair shops use AI to explain faults and quotes clearly: customers get lost when they hear "the power IC is broken", and AI helps say it in everyday words.

Watch videoVideo in Vietnamese

The tech talks, the customer is lost

Customers get lost when the tech says the power IC is broken. AI helps phone repair shops explain faults more simply.

PLAIN WORDS

Use everyday examples

Type the fault in technician terms into a chatbot and ask it to explain with an everyday example.

QUOTE

A clear quote template

Ask AI for a quote template with parts, labor, repair time and warranty, so customers feel at ease.

DATA

Keep customers' phones private

Remind customers to back up before the repair, and never paste their passwords or photos into AI.

Try it now

A fault sheet for the counter

Ask a chatbot for short explanations of the three most common faults, then print them for the counter.
