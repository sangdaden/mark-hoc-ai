What is a token? Why Vietnamese uses more tokens than English

What is a token, and why does asking AI in Vietnamese usually use more tokens than English for the same idea? Mark and Bit explain in under a minute.

Watch videoVideo in Vietnamese

Vietnamese uses more tokens

The same idea written in Vietnamese usually uses more tokens than in English.

TOKEN

AI reads in pieces

AI doesn't read word by word but piece by piece, and each piece is called a token, as video 19 of the channel explained.

TONE MARKS

Words with tone marks get chopped up

Models learn far more English, so Vietnamese words with tone marks often get split into more, smaller pieces.

THE EFFECT

Costs more, remembers less

More tokens means using the API costs more, and long conversations fill the context window faster.
