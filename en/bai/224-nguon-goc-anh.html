Trace the origin of old photos pinned on new stories

Trace old photos pinned on new events: many shocking photos aren't fake, they're real photos of an old story.

Real photo, old story

Many shocking photos aren't fake. They are real photos of an old story pinned on a new event.

REVERSE SEARCH

Where has this photo been

Use an image search tool to see where the photo has appeared before and when it first showed up.

ASK AI

AI spots the details

Send the photo to a chatbot and ask it to list details like shop signs, weather, car models or text on shirts.

COMPARE

Match it to the story

Compare those details with the story attached. AI can guess the place wrong, so treat it only as a lead to keep checking.

Try it now

Trace a hot photo

Pick a photo that's going viral, run a reverse image search, then ask AI to list its details.
