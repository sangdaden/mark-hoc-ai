What is Claude Design? Make slides and web pages without design skills

Have an idea for a landing page or slide deck but can't design?

Watch videoVideo in Vietnamese

Got ideas, can't design

You have an idea for a landing page or a slide deck, but you can't design. Claude Design does that part for you.

CLAUDE DESIGN

Chat on the left, canvas on the right

The screen splits in two: you chat on the left, and the right side is a canvas. You describe it in words, and Claude draws the first version right away.

What can it make?

It can make presentation slides, one-page landing pages, and even prototypes, meaning sample apps you can click through like the real thing.

Edit with words or by hand

Not happy yet? Keep talking: bigger text, a different photo. Or adjust it directly on the canvas. You can also upload photos, Word, PowerPoint or Excel files as material.

DESIGN SYSTEM

Save your brand once

The best part is the design system: save your colors, fonts and logo once, and everything you make afterwards matches your brand automatically.

Where, and which plan?

Claude Design is in beta, for Pro plans and up, and opens on the web or the desktop app.

Try it now

A landing page for your shop

Describe a landing page for your shop or club: the name, three highlights, opening hours, the main color. Edit it twice with words, then send the link to friends for feedback.
