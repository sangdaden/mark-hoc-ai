What are Skills in Claude? Teach Claude a process once, use it forever

Every week you write a report, you paste the same long instructions again?

Watch videoVideo in Vietnamese

Repeating instructions every time

Every week you write a report, and every time you paste the same long set of instructions. Skills let you give the instructions once.

SKILL

A recipe card

A Skill is like a recipe card: the steps, a layout template, sometimes sample files too. Claude keeps it ready.

It takes it out when needed

When what you ask matches the card, Claude takes it out and follows it exactly. You don't need to remind it.

Find it under Customize

Go to Customize in the sidebar. Skills, Connectors and Plugins are all in one place. Click Discover to see the ready-made ones. The free plan can use them too.

CONNECTOR

Bridges to Gmail, Drive, Calendar

Connectors are bridges to Gmail, Google Drive or Google Calendar. Video 32 shows how to give permission safely.

Try it now

Turn on a ready-made skill

Go to Customize, then Skills, and turn on a ready-made skill, like making slides or spreadsheets. Then ask Claude for exactly that task, and see how the result changes.
