Why do you forget after watching a lesson? What interactive learning is

Watched it, forgot it all

You watch a lecture video and feel like you understand everything. The next day someone asks you about it, and your mind goes blank. You're not slow. It's a trap everyone falls into.

THE ILLUSION OF KNOWING

Familiar isn't the same as remembering

When you watch or reread, everything looks familiar, so you think you understand. But recognizing something is different from recalling it yourself.

RETRIEVAL PRACTICE

Recall it yourself

Memory research shows that answering questions yourself helps you remember longer than rereading many times. This is called retrieval practice.

INTERACTIVE LEARNING

Interactive learning

Interactive learning turns you from a watcher into a doer: answer questions in the middle of a lesson, ask about the hard parts, try your own examples.

AI QUIZZES YOU

A study buddy, 24/7

It used to take a teacher sitting next to you to quiz you. Now AI can do that with any video or document you're studying.

Try it now

Pause every 5 minutes

Next time you watch a video to learn, pause every five minutes and say one summary sentence out loud. If you can't, rewind.

Turn videos into lessons

Part two: ask AI to turn any video into a lesson with questions.
