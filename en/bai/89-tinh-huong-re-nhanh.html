Choose-your-own-adventure books

Remember the books where the end of the page said: to open the door, turn to page ten; to run away, turn to page twenty?

BRANCHING SCENARIO

Branching scenarios

In education, this is called a branching scenario. Each choice you make opens up a different outcome.

SEE THE CONSEQUENCES

Don't memorize, see it happen

You don't memorize rules, you see the consequences for yourself. What you say to a customer, and whether they're happy or walk away, sticks with you for a long time.

AI TELLS THE STORY

The story writes itself

Writing a branching scenario used to take a whole week. Now AI is the storyteller: it makes up what happens based on exactly what you choose.

EXAMPLE

A coffee shop

For example: you're the manager of a coffee shop, and a customer complains their drink order was wrong. Four choices appear, you pick one, the customer reacts, and the story continues.

WHAT IT'S FOR

It can teach a lot

It works for teaching customer service, team management, workplace safety, even teaching your kids how to say no to strangers.

A role-playing game
