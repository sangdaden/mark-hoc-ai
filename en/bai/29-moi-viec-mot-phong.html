What are Projects for in AI apps?

A messy chat list: housework, office work, your kid's homework, all mixed together.

Watch videoVideo in Vietnamese

A messy chat list

Last time, we built a prompt library. But the chat list is still a mess: housework, office work, your kid's homework, all mixed together.

One room per task

Many chat apps have a feature called projects. Each project is like a private room for one task.

What's in the room?

Each room has three things: instructions just for that task, the documents you need, and every chat about it.

No more pasting

Open a new chat in a room, and AI has already read that room's instructions and documents. You never have to paste them again.

Nothing gets mixed up

Each room has its own tone and its own set of documents, and nothing spills over.

Try it now

Create your first project

Create a project for a task you do often, like "Family". Write a few lines of instructions and upload two related documents.

Part 4: a research helper
