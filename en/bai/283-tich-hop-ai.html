AI-powered products: how to read the ads with a clear head

AI-powered products, from rice cookers to toothbrushes: read the ads with a clear head, since it's often just an auto mode that's existed for years.

Watch videoVideo in Vietnamese

Everything has AI

From rice cookers to toothbrushes, everything says AI-powered, but often it's just an auto mode that's existed for years.

FIND IT

What does the AI actually do

Look in the ad for one sentence saying exactly what the AI does. If you can't find it, the word AI may just be there to sell.

COMPARE PRICE

What do you get for paying more

Compare with the non-AI version. Ask a chatbot to compare the published specs; don't just read the slogan.

DATA

What the device collects

AI devices often need internet and an account. Read the privacy section to see what data it collects in your home.

Try it now

Inspect one description

Paste the description of a product labeled AI-powered and ask a chatbot which features are real.
