Ten words worth remembering

Across nearly a hundred recent videos, these are the ten AI terms most worth remembering.

TERMS 1-3

Cutoff, limits, versions

One, knowledge cutoff, when AI stopped learning. Two, rate limit, the cap on how many questions you can ask. Three, model version, why AI changes its style.

TERMS 4-6

How AI is taught

Four, RLHF, short for Reinforcement Learning from Human Feedback, meaning AI learns from scores given by real people. Five, sycophancy, the habit of pleasing. Six, overfitting, memorizing instead of understanding.

TERMS 7-10

Hidden instructions and agents

Seven, system prompt, the hidden instruction. Eight, prompt injection, hidden text that gives orders. Nine, jailbreak, getting around safety guardrails. Ten, agent, AI that does several steps on its own.
