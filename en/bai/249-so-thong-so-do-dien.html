Compare air conditioner and fridge specs before you buy

Compare air conditioner and fridge specs with AI before buying: don't get steered to the priciest model by technical jargon.

Watch videoVideo in Vietnamese

Steered to the priciest model

Shopping for an air conditioner, the salesperson steers you to the most expensive one. Photograph the spec sheet and ask AI first.

EXPLAIN

Jargon into plain words

Photograph the spec sheet and ask AI to explain each line, like inverter or BTU capacity, in plain words.

COMPARE

Side by side

Send the specs of two or three models plus your room size and ask AI for a comparison table.

QUESTIONS

Ask the seller back

Ask AI to draft questions about warranty, installation fees and running costs. Check prices at several shops yourself.

Try it now

Decode one label

Photograph the spec label of an appliance at home and ask a chatbot to explain the three most important lines.
