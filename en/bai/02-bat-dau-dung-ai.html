Using AI for the first time

Mark has never used AI and wants to get started in five minutes, with three simple steps.

STEP 1/3

Open a chatbot

Step one: open a free chatbot like Claude, ChatGPT or Gemini, on the web or on your phone.

STEP 2/3

Write like you're talking to a person

Step two: write as if you're talking to a skilled person: who you are, what you need, and what you want the result to look like.

STEP 3/3

Not happy? Keep replying

Step three: not happy yet? Reply again, shorter, add an example, and AI fixes it right in the chat.

STAY SAFE

Don't paste private information

Remember: never paste passwords, account numbers or personal documents into AI.

Try it now

This week's menu

Try it now: ask AI to plan this week's dinners for a family of four on a budget of 500,000 dong.
