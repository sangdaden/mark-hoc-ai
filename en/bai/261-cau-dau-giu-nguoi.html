30-second scripts: a first line that keeps viewers watching

Write a 30-second short-video script with AI: the first line decides whether viewers scroll past or stay.

Watch videoVideo in Vietnamese

A few seconds decide

Viewers decide to scroll or stay within the first few seconds, so the first line matters most.

FIRST LINE

Three good ways to open

Mark học AI opens with a result, a paradox or a common mistake, never a long greeting.

MANY VERSIONS

Ask for five openers

Give your video idea to a chatbot, an AI chat assistant, ask for five opening lines, and pick the one that makes you want to keep listening.

TRIM

One idea, one action

Thirty seconds only fits a few short sentences, so ask AI to cut the extras and keep one idea and one thing to try.

Try it now

Score your opener

Write the opening line for a video you plan to make, ask a chatbot to score it and rewrite it three ways.
