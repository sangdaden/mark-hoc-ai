How to ask AI in Vietnamese so it understands you

How to ask AI in Vietnamese so it gets it right the first time: typing without tone marks confuses AI more than you think. Three habits.

Watch videoVideo in Vietnamese

Typing without tone marks

Type Vietnamese without tone marks, and AI misunderstands more easily than you think.

TONE MARKS

Always type the tone marks

The word ban without tone marks could mean table, sell, friend or busy. Type all the marks and AI doesn't have to guess.

CONTEXT

Say you're in Vietnam

Say clearly that you're in Vietnam, so AI uses the right currency, laws and units, instead of assuming you're in the US.

FORMS OF ADDRESS

Say how to address people

When writing to your boss or messaging a customer, say which form of address to use, like the polite words for older people or valued customers, so the writing sounds natural.
