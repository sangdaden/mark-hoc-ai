What is AGI

Many AI companies say they'll soon build AGI, but so far nobody agrees on exactly what AGI is.

Today's AI is unevenly smart

Today's chatbots are great at writing, translating and coding, yet sometimes miscount the letters in a simple sentence.

AGI

Most mental work

AGI, short for artificial general intelligence, is usually understood as AI that can do most mental work at a human level.

DEBATE

How do you measure it?

The hard part is how to measure it: by exams, by work that earns money, or by the ability to learn new things on its own like people do.

Try it now

Read AGI news with a clear head

When you see news that AGI is coming soon, ask three things: who said it, what they mean by AGI, and how they measure it.
