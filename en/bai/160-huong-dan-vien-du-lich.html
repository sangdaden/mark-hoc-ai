AI for tour guides: itineraries and storytelling

AI for tour guides: planning itineraries, telling stories and answering guests at once, and AI can take on part of the preparation.

Watch videoVideo in Vietnamese

A busy tour guide

A tour guide plans the itinerary, tells stories and answers every guest question, and AI can take on part of the preparation.

ITINERARY

Itineraries for each group

Tell AI whether the group is older people, families with small kids, or young people who love walking, and ask for an itinerary that suits each group's energy.

STORIES

Prepare your stories

Ask AI for ways to tell the place's history in a lively way, but check dates and names in official sources.

LANGUAGE

Translating when needed

With guests who speak a less common language, use a voice translation app for basic conversation, but give them the important things in writing.

Try it now

Three opening lines

Pick a place you often take guests, ask AI for three fun opening lines for three different groups, then check every fact.
