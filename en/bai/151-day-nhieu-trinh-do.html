Differentiated teaching with AI: strong and struggling students in one class

Differentiated teaching with AI: in one class, some finish in five minutes and some still don't get the task, and there's only one teacher.

Watch videoVideo in Vietnamese

One class, three levels

In one class, some students finish in five minutes, some still don't understand the task, and there's only one teacher.

THREE VERSIONS

One exercise, three versions

Ask AI to rewrite the same exercise in three versions: one with step-by-step hints, a standard one, and an advanced one for fast students.

EXPLAINING

Many ways to explain

For students who don't get it, ask AI for another way to explain: with a drawing, an everyday example, or a short story.

IN CHARGE

The teacher stays in charge

The teacher chooses which version goes to which student, because only the teacher knows what each student needs.

Try it now

Try it with two groups

Take a hard exercise from this week, ask AI for a hint version and an advanced version, then try them with two groups.
