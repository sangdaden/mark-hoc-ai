Day 5: reviewing your spending

Day five of seven: money keeps disappearing and you don't know where. AI can find the leaks from a simple spending list.

REMOVE NUMBERS

Remove account numbers first

Copy your expenses: date, item and amount. Delete account numbers, card numbers and recipient names before pasting.

GROUP

Group and total

Ask AI to group them into food, transport and shopping, then total each group.

LEAKS

Find two leaks

Ask AI to point out the two small expenses that repeat the most. AI helps you see clearly; it doesn't give investment advice.

Try it now

Day 5 task

Paste the last seven days of spending into a chatbot, ask it to group and total them, then pick one thing to cut.
