Read a rental contract with AI: deposit, penalties, repairs

Read your rental contract with AI before signing: many people lose their deposit because of one small line.

Watch videoVideo in Vietnamese

Losing a deposit over one line

Many people lose their deposit over a single line in the lease. AI helps you check before you sign.

HIDE INFO

Remove names and ID numbers

Photograph the contract and cover both sides' names, ID numbers and bank account numbers before pasting into AI.

THREE SPOTS

Deposit, penalties, repairs

Ask AI to summarize three things: deposit return terms, penalties for moving out early, and who pays for repairs.

ASK BACK

Make vague parts clear

For vague parts, ask AI to draft questions for the landlord so they're written into the contract. For big disputes, ask a lawyer.

Try it now

A photo checklist

Ask a chatbot for a checklist of photos to take on move-in day, as evidence when you get your deposit back.
