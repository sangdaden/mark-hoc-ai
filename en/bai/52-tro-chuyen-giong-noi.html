How does voice chat with AI work? Voice mode explained

Talking with AI like calling a friend. But inside, there are two very different ways to do it.

▶ Watch videoVideo in Vietnamese

Talking with your voice

Last time, AI read text out loud. Now we put listening and talking together. You ask out loud, and AI answers out loud, like calling a friend. But inside, there are two very different ways to do it.

THE OLD WAY

Three machines in a row

The old way chains three machines: one transcribes your words into text, AI writes a reply, and a third machine reads it out loud. Each stage takes a moment, so you wait a few seconds in total.

THE OLD WAY

Your tone gets lost

Worse, once it becomes text, your tone of voice disappears. You say "yeah, fine" in a sad voice, AI only sees those two words, and answers cheerfully.

THE NEW WAY

One brain that listens and talks

The new way uses one model: it hears the sound directly and speaks sound directly, without turning it into text. It can notice a sad voice or laughter, and answer in a tone that fits you.

FAST

Answers in about half a second

With fewer stages, AI can answer in about half a second, close to the pace of human conversation. You can even interrupt: if you cut in, it stops to listen.

NOTE

A warm voice isn't always right

But a natural-sounding voice doesn't mean the information is right. For important things like medicine or money, still double-check.

Try it now

Practice interviews with AI

Turn on voice mode and say: "Play an interviewer, and help me fix my pronunciation". The same works for practicing a new language: if you get it wrong, just try again. Nobody's laughing.
