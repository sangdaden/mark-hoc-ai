Viral health news

Health claims like "drink this and cure that" are usually wrong, yet they get shared the fastest.

RED FLAGS

Three suspicious signs

Three red flags: promises of a quick cure, no clear source, and pressure to share it right away.

ASK AI

Let AI split the claims

Remove the sender's name, paste the message into a chatbot, an AI chat assistant, and ask it to split out each claim to check.

SOURCES

Doctors make the call

Then check the health authority's or a major hospital's website. AI helps you check; how to treat it is up to a doctor.

Check one group message
