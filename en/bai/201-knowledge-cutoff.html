AI doesn't know yesterday's news

Ask AI about yesterday's news and it answers wrong while sounding very sure. The culprit is called the knowledge cutoff.

CUTOFF DATE

The data cutoff

It's the date the training data stops: the last point the model, the AI's brain, learned from.

WHY

Trained first, released later

After training, a model still has to be tested before release, so the cutoff is usually a few months old.

WHAT TO DO

Turn on web search or paste the news

For recent news, turn on web search or paste the article for AI to read, then check the publication date.

Try it now

Ask for the cutoff date

Ask a chatbot for its knowledge cutoff date, then ask about something from this week.
