Ask AI to write your weekly review and pick one habit to keep

Day 7: looking back

Day seven of seven, the last one: if you finish without looking back, it's easy to slip into old habits next week.

RECAP

Quickly recap the last six days

Type out for AI which days worked, which you skipped and which task helped most. Messy is fine.

SUMMARY

A five-line review

Ask AI to turn it into a five-line review: what went well, what got in the way, and what you want to try next.

KEEP ONE

Keep just one habit

Then choose only one habit to keep. Keeping one thing for a long time beats dropping all seven after a week.

Try it now

Day 7 task

Ask a chatbot to turn your notes into a weekly review, then pick one habit and set a calendar reminder.
