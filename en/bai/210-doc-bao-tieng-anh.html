A dictionary for every line

Reading English news and looking up words on every line is discouraging. AI can rewrite the article at your level.

REWRITE

Easier, nothing missing

Paste a paragraph, tell AI your level, and ask it to rewrite it with easier words while keeping every point.

Easy first, original second

Read the easy version first, then the original. You'll guess many words without looking them up.

FIVE WORDS

Learn only five words

At the end, ask AI to choose just the five words most worth learning, with meanings and an example sentence.
