How AI demo videos differ from real life: check for yourself

Every AI demo video looks smooth because it's the best run picked out: here's how to check for yourself before you believe it.

Watch videoVideo in Vietnamese

Smooth demo, bumpy reality

Every AI demo video looks smooth because it's the best run picked out, sometimes even edited together.

WATCH CLOSELY

Look for the cuts

Watch for scene cuts, fast-forwarding, and the small print in the bottom corner of the screen.

TRY IT

Use your own task

The surest way is to test it with your own real task, not the exact demo example. Try three times to see if results are consistent.

WAIT FOR REVIEWS

Wait for real users

If a feature isn't open to everyone yet, wait a few weeks and read real users' reviews before paying.

Try it now

One task to test

Think back to an AI demo you just watched and write down one task of your own to test it with.
