Every job is a bundle of tasks

Last time, we saw that every job is a bundle of tasks. So which tasks will AI take first?

SIGN 1

Repeated again and again

Sign one: it repeats. It's the same kind of thing every day, like entering invoices or answering customers' usual questions.

SIGN 2

It fits on a screen

Sign two: the work fits on a screen, as words, numbers or pictures. Hands-on work in a physical place is much harder for AI to reach.

SIGN 3

Clear right and wrong

Sign three: the result is clearly right or wrong and easy to check. Work that needs reading feelings or weighing many sides still stays mostly with people.

A look at a few jobs

Look at a few jobs: accountant, salesperson, office worker, teacher. Every one has parts AI takes first, and parts that still need you.
