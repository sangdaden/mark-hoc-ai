Ask a question, or hand over a task?

Chat means ask one thing, get one answer. Cowork is different: it's an AI agent. You hand over a whole task, then go do something else.

COWORK

Describe the result, Claude makes the plan

You describe the result you want. Claude makes a plan on its own, splits it into steps, and works through them one by one.

Many steps

It reads your files, searches the web for more, builds spreadsheets with formulas, and makes slides. Many small tasks even run at the same time.

Close your laptop, the work keeps going

The work runs in the cloud, so you can close your laptop and it keeps going. You can follow along on the web, the desktop app or your phone.

You're still at the wheel

For sensitive steps like sending emails or deleting files, read the plan and approve it yourself first. You hand over the work, but you're still the one driving.

Which plan? On a schedule?

Cowork needs a paid plan, Pro and up. To have a task run on a schedule every week, use a scheduled task, as in video 31.

Try it now

Three options, one recommendation

Gather three quotes or three options you're torn between, and ask Cowork to make a comparison table and recommend one, explaining why. Then double-check the numbers.
