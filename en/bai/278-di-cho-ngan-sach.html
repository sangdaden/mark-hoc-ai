Make a budget grocery list with AI

Make a budget grocery list with AI, because shopping without a list is the fastest way to overspend.

Watch videoVideo in Vietnamese

Overspending at the market

Shopping without a list is the fastest way to overspend, buy too much and waste it.

BUDGET

Say the amount

Tell a chatbot your budget for the week, how many people eat and the family's favorite dishes. Ask for a list sorted by section.

REAL PRICES

AI doesn't know market prices

AI doesn't know today's prices. Write down prices for a few things you buy often, and ask it to total them and show where to cut.

SWAP

Switch to cheaper dishes

For anything over budget, ask AI for a cheaper option the family will still enjoy, like swapping beef for eggs or tofu.

Try it now

A list for tomorrow

Write down tomorrow's grocery budget and ask AI for a list that fits it.
