    6 questions before adopting an AI tool
    

    The three ★ questions must be Yes.
    


    📜Licence ★Approved for this exact use?
    🗄️Data ★Does patient data leave the hospital or get used for training?
    📊EvidenceIndependent research, not just vendor figures?
    🇻🇳Local fitTested on patients and records like ours?
    🔌IntegrationWorks with the hospital software you use?
    ⚖️Accountability ★Who is responsible when it's wrong, and where are incidents reported?
