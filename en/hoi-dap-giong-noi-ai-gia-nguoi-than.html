Can AI fake a relative's voice to ask for money?

Answered in a minute, with the warning signs and what to do right now.

Short answer

Yes, a few seconds is enough

Yes. A few seconds of clear audio from a posted video, a voice note or a strange phone call is enough for voice-cloning software to make a convincing copy. Your ears can't keep up, so trust a routine instead: ask for the family safe word, hang up, and call back on the saved number.

Do this

More questions

People also ask

What is a family safe word?

A secret word or phrase your family agrees on and never posts online. When someone calls asking for urgent money, ask for it: a scammer can copy a voice but can't know the word.

Can I tell an AI voice apart if I listen carefully?

Less and less. In the channel's test, two identical sentences were both read by AI and many people still guessed one was human. Don't bet money on your ears.

How do scammers get my voice?

From public videos with your voice, voice notes, or a strange call where you only said hello, who is it, I'm listening.
