Free game · The three eyes of AI

Night drive

Get a self-driving car home through sun, night, rain and fog. When the weather changes, change the eye: pick the wrong one and people, bikes and red lights vanish.

How to play: ← → or A D to change lanes, hold Space or ↓ to brake, keys 1 2 3 switch on LiDAR, radar, camera. On a phone the game goes full screen in landscape: hold the phone sideways and use the on-screen buttons. At a red light, hold Brake until it turns green.
