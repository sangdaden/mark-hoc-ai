      About the channel

      Mark học AI (“Mark learns AI”) makes videos for non-experts: office workers, teachers, students, shop owners. In each video, Mark asks the question you're probably wondering about too, and Bit the robot explains it with simple animations.

      Every video comes in two versions: vertical for TikTok, Instagram Reels, Facebook Reels and YouTube Shorts, and horizontal for YouTube. Videos in a series are numbered “Part X/N” so you always know where you are. The videos are in Vietnamese; the articles on this site are in English.
