How it works

  1. 1

    You send the content

    Topic, key points, product photos or logo. Not sure yet? Message us and we will pick a topic together.

  2. 2

    We send the script

    Narration and a scene-by-scene outline. We only start the video once you approve it.

  3. 3

    We make the video

    Animation, voiceover, subtitles, and a word-by-word check of the narration.

  4. 4

    Delivery and edits

    We send the MP4 for review, edit within your plan's rounds, and you post it right away.
