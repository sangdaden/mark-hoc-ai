Three things the model struggles with

Reservoir releases

Release schedules aren't in the rain data.

Broken dikes

Flash floods on streams

Three takeaways

1Rain first, flood later

Water takes days to travel downstream; that gap is the warning time.

2Learned from thousands of gauges

Learning from 5,000+ gauges lets it forecast rivers that have none.

3Official alerts come first

Dam releases, dike breaks, flash floods: follow the weather agency and local authorities.
