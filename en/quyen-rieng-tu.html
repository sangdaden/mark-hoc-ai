Privacy

What the site counts and stores on your device

Mark học AI only counts numbers to learn which content helps. Your learning progress stays on your device.

Last updated: Oct 18, 2026

What the site sends

To learn which content helps, the site counts page views and a few actions such as opening YouTube, reading to the end, finishing a quiz or tapping 👍/👎 on a station. Each one is just a number with a few general labels: which page, Vietnamese or English, phone or computer, where you came from, which country. The site uses Umami, with no cookies and no way to identify individuals. If you turn on “Do Not Track”, nothing is counted.

The site does not collect your name, email, phone number, answers or anything you type (Ask Bit, the glossary, the name on a certificate). Nothing is sold or shared with advertisers.

What is stored on your device

To remember your progress without an account, your browser stores a few things (localStorage). They stay on your device; the site cannot read them remotely:

  • Display: light/dark theme and language (mhai-theme, mhai-lang).
  • Learning progress: finished days, watched videos, stations done, review questions, days you studied, pages opened, quiz and game results, your chosen job, reminder time (mhai-lo-trinh, mhai-da-xem, mhai-tram, mhai-on, mhai-hoat-dong, mhai-da-mo, mhai-kiem-tra, mhai-xep, mhai-nganh, mhai-nhac…).
  • The name on your certificate if you type one (mhai-ten). It is also included in the progress code if you copy one from your profile.
  • Your last visit (mhai-do-lan-cuoi), to count returning visitors without cookies.
  • A few small session notes (sessionStorage) that disappear when you close the tab.

Outside services

Fonts load from Google Fonts, so Google sees your IP address when they load. Embedded videos use YouTube's youtube-nocookie mode; when you press play, YouTube's own policy applies.
